# Research and development

Start with [the current state](SESSION-RESUME.md) and use
[WAVE_PLAYBOOK](docs/WAVE_PLAYBOOK.md) for the development cycle and tool owners.
The user-facing contract is [one codec-target score](docs/CODEC_TARGET_METRIC.md).

A hypothesis becomes an admitted model only when its complete scorer serves in
Rust through a zensim API and evaluation uses that API. Python remains useful
for invention and independent references. Scientific correctness, held-out
quality and product qualification require separate evidence.

Before choosing data, read [DATA_SPLITS](docs/DATA_SPLITS.md),
[DATASET_HISTORY](docs/DATASET_HISTORY.md) and the shared
[provenance index](../DATA_PROVENANCE.md). Before fundamental method changes,
check `../zenpapers` and its later summaries. Feature IDs and revisions are
specified in [FEATURE_SET_IDS](docs/FEATURE_SET_IDS.md).

The [May research guide](docs/history/RESEARCH-may-2026.md) preserves old
corpus tables, recipes and provisional calibrations. Its ship names, paths,
training/evaluation split suggestions and performance numbers are historical.
The [cleanup audit](benchmarks/science_workflow_audit_2026-09-07.md) records
transcript/memory chronology and implementation-confidence limits.
