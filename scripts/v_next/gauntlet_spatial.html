What failed, and what this does not prove

M3f is the recorded Spearman correlation between predicted repair gains and actual score changes. Its diagnostic bar is ≥ 0.70. M2 compares feature-space linearization with those actual changes; its bar is ≥ 0.99. A check fails either bar; missing values are never a pass.

The packet has seven image/corruption cases, 20 independently fitted layouts and three block sizes. These are seed4004 development models, trained on a small JXL metric-proxy packet. The swap example is a whole-image R/B swap, not all permutations. Salt-and-pepper and aliasing cases reuse pinned training fixtures. The maps show block repair gains, not pixel error or perceptual importance. A high correlation does not guarantee calibrated gain magnitude.

No candidate is qualified by these checks. They measure reference-pixel repairs, not native codec rate/distortion benefit. Color and contrast are unmodified; enlarged images use nearest-neighbor display.
