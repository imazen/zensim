# zensim champion Pareto frontier — 2026-05-11

**Targets** (from `zensim/CLAUDE.md`):
- **CID22 SROCC > 0.8934** (gold standard, held-out human MOS)
- **Non-monotonic q-step rate < 4.86%** (V0_2 floor; smoothness)

## TL;DR

**No bake in the current 228-feature single-layer recipe space dual-clears both targets.**

- **V0_5 shipped** (`v0_4_2026-04-30.bin`, md5 `bb7e24a16a64afa43eb296bf151fb6b8`) is the **only verified bake meeting the smoothness target**. CID22 0.8893 / non-mono 4.57%.
- **CID22 0.8934 has never been measured on a held-out bake.** It was an aspirational target carried in `MEMORY.md` and `CLAUDE.md` references; a fresh full-dataset re-eval of V0_5 (the file MEMORY.md attributed 0.8934 to) lands at 0.8893.
- The Pareto frontier in the KonJND-aligned recipe family is bounded at approximately `CID22 ≈ 0.8893 − 0.001·TV` with non-mono growing from 5.1% (TV=10) to 5.5% (TV=30). h=128 vs h=64 makes no detectable difference once seed variance is accounted for.

## What did NOT work

1. **Doubling capacity (h=128 vs h=64)** with TV=30 KonJND-aligned → CID22 0.8803 (WORSE than h=64 TV=20). Capacity ≠ improvement when over-regularized.
2. **Adding KonJND-aligned data to V0_5's recipe** → +0.10 KADID, +0.10 TID, but CID22 stays ≤ 0.8921 (best case) and non-mono moves up.
3. **TV regularization** → trades CID22 for smoothness on a strict line.
4. **WebP-mono recipe** (Tick 62) → CID22 0.8941 (target!) but non-mono 6.72%, far from smoothness target.

## Product implication of per-band data

The aggregate-CID22 frame (V0_5 0.8893 > TV=5 0.8880 by 0.0013) **understates the difference**.

- For users typing **zensim 70** (B2 high quality): V0_5 and TV=5 are within 0.001 of each other on CID22; KonJND lifts TID/KADID at this band by +0.13-0.18.
- For users typing **zensim 55** (B1 medium): KonJND substantially better (TID +0.28, KADID +0.07).
- For users typing **zensim 40** (B0 below medium): KonJND massively better (KADID +0.22, TID +0.15) — but CID22 narrowly favors V0_5 (+0.02).

The **smoothness gap (V0_5 4.57% vs KonJND-mix 5.1-5.5%) is what currently keeps V0_5 shipping** — without that gap, KonJND-mix would arguably be the better product despite the −0.001 aggregate CID22.

## Recommendations

1. **Accept V0_5 as the achievable Pareto frontier** in this recipe space. The shipped bake is in production already.
2. **Re-anchor the CID22 target** in `CLAUDE.md` to either:
   - `0.8893` (V0_5 baseline, measurement-backed), or
   - `V0_5 + 0.005 = 0.8943` (smallest meaningful lift, formally aspirational).
3. **For future CID22 > 0.8934**, the realistic paths are:
   - **Feature-space extension** — add new image features outside the current 228 (queued in zentrain/INVERSION.md tier 2-4).
   - **Different model class** — multi-head, FiLM, MoE (zentrain phase 4 work).
   - These are NOT recipe-tuning problems within the existing trainer.
