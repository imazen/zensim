## Addressability definition and active mentor

A7r groups finite candidate values in the bound grid by image and codec, orders its quality-oriented q,
selects the first point and then the earliest next point whose **absolute mentor
score difference from the last selected point is at least 0.5**, until four
points (K+1, K=3) have been selected. Short windows are excluded and counted; none are too short on either bound grid (106 standard and 182 ladder windows).
A candidate represents the window only if each of its three edges strictly
increases and it does not share an instrument-wide minimum clamp. Clamp tolerance
is 1e-9; a sole minimum-holder ladder is exempt. Candidate ties are exact;
0.5 is the mentor window margin, not a candidate tie tolerance.

The bar is the mentor's own represented fraction computed on the same cells and
selected windows. A7r requires zero codec fractions below their live mentor bars.
The active mentor is the registered `peer_ssim2` same-cell score table, rather
than the historical model named in the floor registry annotation. Owner:
[FloorMeasure](../zensim-validate/src/dial_addressability.rs).
