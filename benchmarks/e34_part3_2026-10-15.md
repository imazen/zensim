## Definitive P2 on the fitted smoke

[P2 receipt](e34_part3_p2_2026-10-15.json) and
[135-case table](e34_part3_p2_2026-10-15.csv) bind the actual packed L smoke.
The coefficient convention is `raw = pin - dot(c, x)`, so the comparison is
`s` against `-c`. Coefficients come from the loaded quantized weights and
scales: `c_i = -w2 * w1_i / scale_i`. They describe the algebraic linear head;
the instrument measures the existing finite-difference serving implementation.

The maximum `|s + c|` is `0.4817685172203939`, at `I41_10_05`, block 8,
feature slot 632: `s = -0.57220458984375`, `c = 0.09043607262335611`.
Thus P2 does not establish exact equality of these sensitivities and the
coefficients. No all-zero sensitivity case occurs. The negative-input case
list is **empty**: zero negative rows among 55,515 base/repair rows, checked
over the loaded model's declared inputs. Full `s` and `c` arrays are retained
in the hash-pinned artifact `part3/p2-l.json`.

The raw diagnostic also records 134/135 at the steering bars, with the same
`broad-199-b64` failure and zero feature-replay disagreements. This diagnostic
does not replace the separate served verdict. No result-driven tuning or
change to a gate, epsilon, weight or threshold was made.

The raw packet was built with `just e34-linear-probe packet`, using the
original hash-pinned `steerfix-2026-10-09/full-packet.json`, the packed L model,
`part3/p2-l.json` as result and `part3/p2-l-packet.json` as packet output.
