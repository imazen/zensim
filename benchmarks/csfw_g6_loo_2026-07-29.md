## Honest caveats

1. **This is an SDR-ranking instrument.** The twin mix contains no HDR leg,
   so the gate cannot price the cross-route (cbrt↔PU) commensurability the
   weighting was designed for and G1 measured (+0.07..+0.10 cross-route
   SROCC on DMEAN). G6 was pre-registered as the *training-side* criterion
   precisely to test whether the head can use the lanes on SDR data — it
   can't (and is hurt by the contrast twins). The HDR-side question
   belongs to stage-2/chroma-tier adjudication on the re-extracted HDR
   corpora, per the gates doc's own sequencing.
2. The imazen26 legs (2 of e2's pinned 8) have no 956 extraction; the Σ
   runs on the 6 available pinned legs (~94% of the 720-era condemning
   signal per the bandvis calibration).
3. KADID/TID stay train-guards; the clean-holdout-only Σ (+0.0377) and the
   guard-inclusive Σ (+0.0608) agree in sign.
4. Zero-ablation LOO measures marginal value *within this BVLS fit* (no
   refit) — identical semantics to the e2/944 numbers it is compared
   against.
5. The gates-doc blanket "CGAIN std ~1e-5 R-class" caveat is corrected
   here to aic3-specific (see fire-rate instrumentation); this run's
   verdict does not rest on rare-fire legs.
6. λ_b ≡ 1 / κ_Y = 1.0 as shipped (falsifier 2's honest-stop) — this gate
   adjudicates the lanes as landed, not the κ·λ family.
