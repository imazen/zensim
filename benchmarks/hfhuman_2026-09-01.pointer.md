The recovered AIC-3 IPTC leg (doc APPENDIX A). Self-contained: it re-runs the
whole arm pipeline into its own directory, so it also carries regenerated copies
of the three original arms as the byte-identity gate (`ptc_native` +
`btc_displayed` `pairs.tsv` sha256-identical to the parent, all 305
`native_btc/` crops byte-identical).
