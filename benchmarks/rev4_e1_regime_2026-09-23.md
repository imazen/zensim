## MISSING / not done

- **GMSD: no rows on any E1 corpus.** The gmsd lane (unmerged) scored KADID SELECT and KonFiG only.
- **MS-SSIM and PSNR** exist only as the AIC-4 organisers' crop columns; **DSSIM** only on
  CID22, AIC-3 and AIC-4 crop (site parquets). No other corpus has them.
- **CVVDP at its documented display** exists only for AIC-4 crop (`standard_fhd`, cvvdpfix lane,
  unmerged). Every other CVVDP row is our port at `standard_4k`: not its best configuration.
- **AIC-4 full resolution** has only SSIMULACRA2 and butteraugli as peers.
- **R915 fast/rich have no CSIQ or SDR25 rows; B and D have no SDR25 rows on the q_jnd table**
  (their only SDR25 reads use the 372-root table whose orientation is unverified). PreviewV0_2
  has CID22-A, AIC-3 and AIC-4 only. D, R915 and V0_2 have no forced-choice scores.
- **Forced choice has SSIMULACRA2 as its only peer**, so its "best peer" is SSIMULACRA2 by default.
- **LIVE excluded** (registered target defect, DATA_SPLITS §8.2).
- **The stats binary was reused, not rebuilt:** the fresh build waited on the shared heavy lock and
  was cancelled. The reused `panel` (sha256 `f11857c2…`, primary checkout, 2026-09-19) matches
  main's panel sources (unchanged since 2026-09-14) and reproduces 21 recorded board SROCCs to 4
  decimals (`/var/tmp/rev4-e1/sanity_global.log`).
- KonJND has no JND bands: every stimulus sits at its source's PJND, so the whole corpus is the
  near-threshold band and there is no mid or low band to compare against.

## Decision-rule outcome

**Near-threshold concentration is REFUTED for B (the served default) and for the best candidate C
(`W10L9PH_s4004`), under the primary rule and all three sensitivity variants.** It is refuted for
D, R915 fast and PreviewV0_2 too. R915 rich is refuted on the primary rule and flips to unresolved
only when the JPEG-AIC family is counted per member.

- **B:** the only near-threshold deficit is on the JPEG-AIC family (AIC-3 −0.047, AIC-4 crop
  −0.067, AIC-4 full −0.064, forced-choice HF −0.0050). B loses just as much in the mid and low
  bands of the same family (AIC-3 MID −0.076, LOW −0.042; AIC-4 crop MID −0.095, LOW −0.086;
  forced-choice MF −0.0040, LF −0.0014), and it collapses on CSIQ's lowest band (Q1 −0.191).
  On CID22-A B ties SSIMULACRA2 in every band. T = 1 unit (JPEG-AIC); L = 2 (JPEG-AIC, CSIQ).
- **C:** no near-threshold deficit anywhere (CID22-A Q5 −0.0060 [−0.0146, +0.0021]; CSIQ Q5
  −0.022 n.s.; KonJND −0.028 n.s.; AIC-3 NT +0.004; AIC-4 crop NT −0.002; forced-choice HF
  +0.0024 [−0.0002, +0.0056]). Its significant deficits all sit **below** the top band: CID22-A
  Q3 −0.012, Q2 −0.019, Q1 −0.016 (all vs SSIMULACRA2); AIC-4 crop LOW −0.066 (vs CVVDP-fhd);
  forced-choice LF −0.0007. T = 0; L = 2.

What the losing bands share (exploratory, below): the deficits are **cross-codec**
(cross-distortion) ordering errors. Same-codec ladder order is near-perfect for every metric in
every band, and zensim ties there.

## Exploratory (not preregistered): what the losing bands share

Each band's within-reference pairs are split into same-codec pairs (on CSIQ, same distortion type)
and cross-codec pairs. The draws and the statistic are the registered ones.

Full split table (every band, B/C vs SSIMULACRA2 and vs the best peer, with CIs):
`benchmarks/rev4_e1_regime_2026-09-23_explore.md`.

Readings:
- **Same-codec order is solved.** Every metric orders a single codec's ladder almost perfectly, and
  C's Δ is 0.0000 there in every band. The deficits live in **cross-codec** comparisons: C on
  CID22-A Q1/Q2 (−0.019/−0.022 vs SSIMULACRA2), C and B vs CVVDP-fhd on AIC-4 crop LOW
  (−0.080/−0.104).
- **B has a low-quality ladder inversion on CSIQ.** In CSIQ Q1, B misorders 36 of 62 same-type
  pairs: BLUR 11/12, JPEG2000 16/21, JPEG 9/22. SSIMULACRA2, C and D misorder 0 of them. B's
  scores there are 1.9–29.8 (worklog step 5). This is the 2026-07-07 bake on the 2026-08-30 root,
  **not** the served by-id bake on the runtime root. Re-check it on the served bytes before calling
  it a product bug.
- B's near-threshold losses on the JPEG-AIC family are also cross-codec (AIC-3 NT −0.030, AIC-4
  crop NT −0.062 vs SSIMULACRA2). B's same-codec order is intact.

## Caveats

- **Few clusters.** The JPEG-AIC family has 10 (AIC-3) or 5 (AIC-4, SDR25, forced choice)
  references. Percentile bootstraps over 5 clusters understate uncertainty. Several family-level
  "deficits" (for example C on AIC-4 crop LOW) rest on 5 clusters and 73 stimuli.
- **Best peer chosen per band by point estimate** favours the peer, in every band equally.
- **Eras and identities differ by corpus.** B is `MT914_matched_B` (by-id bake, rev1 root) on
  CID22/AIC-3/KonJND; the 2026-07-07 bake on the 2026-08-30 root on CSIQ; the profile pixel read on
  AIC-4; and the 2026-07-07 bake (v1 regime, 2026-09-01) in forced choice. D uses
  `D_shipped@dguard2` on CSIQ. PreviewV0_2 on CID22/AIC-3 is the May-era site parquet (raw
  distance, negated; orientation set label-free, SROCC −0.99 vs SSIMULACRA2). No table mixes two
  identities within one column.
- **zensim was trained on SSIMULACRA2 targets.** Ties with SSIMULACRA2 are partly distillation.
- **CSIQ distortions (noise, blur, contrast) are also KADID/TID families** that zensim and
  SSIMULACRA2 both trained or tuned on.
- **Exposure.** Every model here has prior exposure to these populations (DATA_SPLITS ledger). The
  CID22-B targets were touched by one element-wise equality check before the prereg, disclosed in
  its §1, and never binned or used.
- **KonJND's construct caveat** (L03-10) applies: SROCC at threshold partly rewards sensitivity to
  physical distortion magnitude.
- **SDR25's within-reference ladder order is 1.000 for most metrics.** It cannot discriminate.
- AIC-3 targets are the CTC design values (−0.25 × level); 121 of 600 are interpolated.
