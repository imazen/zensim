## 4. Bands (functions of the target only; identical for every metric)

- **JND corpora** (AIC-3, AIC-4 crop/full, SDR25), JND distance `d = −t`:
  **NT** (near-threshold) `d < 1`; **MID** `1 ≤ d < 2`; **LOW** `d ≥ 2`.
- **MOS corpora** (CID22-A, CSIQ): quintiles of `t` over the corpus population
  (numpy `percentile` 20/40/60/80, linear); Q5 = highest quality = **NT**; Q4, Q3 = **MID**
  (reported separately); Q2, Q1 = **LOW** (reported separately). A value equal to an edge
  goes to the lower band; the minimum goes to Q1.
- **KonJND-404**: every stimulus sits at its source's PJND by construction, so the whole
  corpus is one **NT** band. It has no MID/LOW band.
- **Forced choice**: fidelity band by the more distorted side of the triplet,
  `m = max(dlevel_left, dlevel_right)`: **HF** `m ≤ 3` (= NT), **MF** `4–6`, **LF** `7–10`;
  crossed with question type `vs_original` / `same_codec` / `cross_codec` and `all`.
  Trap and bias rows excluded (as hfhuman).

## 5. Statistics (owner: `panel` in `zensim-validate`, via `scripts/lib/zen_stats.py` and `panel --pairwise`)

Per corpus × band × metric:

1. **Pairwise ordering accuracy (primary, all units except KonJND).** Within-reference
   pairs whose two stimuli both lie in the band and whose targets differ (exact target
   ties dropped). Rows to `panel --pairwise` as `group = reference`, `s_left/s_right` =
   metric quality, `choice` = the side with the lower `t` (more distorted), weight 1.
   Metric ties score 0.5 (owner rule). Statistic = `acc_response`.
2. **Band SROCC ("tail SROCC", secondary; primary for KonJND).** Spearman of metric vs
   `t` over all stimuli in the band, pooled across references, orientation-aligned
   (sign kept: a reversed ranking is negative). From `panel --batch --stats srocc`.
3. **Forced choice (primary for U4e).** `panel --pairwise` on the raw responses,
   `group = question_id`, response-weighted `acc_response`, plus the ceiling. The decision
   cell per fidelity band is question type `all`; the question-type splits are secondary.
   The only eligible peer is SSIMULACRA2. `btc_displayed` and the native PTC+IPTC legs are
   secondary readings of the same responses.
4. **Global context.** Whole-corpus SROCC and whole-corpus within-reference pairwise
   accuracy per metric.

**Uncertainty.** Reference-clustered bootstrap, **B = 2000, seed 20260923**: each resample
draws the corpus's references with replacement (count = number of references); the same
draws are used for every metric and band of a corpus (paired). Band SROCC uses the rows of
the drawn references (with multiplicity); pairwise accuracy uses `--resample` group lists
of the drawn references. Forced choice: image-clustered (5 or 10 images), same seed;
the triplet-clustered interval is reported as secondary. Percentile 95 % CIs.
Paired differences `Δ = zensim − peer` per resample.

n reported for every cell: stimuli, pairs (or responses), references.

## 6. Decision rule (copied from the program doc, then made operational)

> Confirmed if zensim's deficit to the best peer in the top-quality band(s) excludes zero on
> at least two corpora, while its mid and low bands tie or win. Refuted if the deficits are
> uniform or sit in the low-quality bands. Anything else is unresolved.

Operational, per zensim model M, on the primary statistic:

- **Best peer** of a (unit, band): the eligible peer (§3) with the highest point estimate in
  that band; its Δ CI is the paired bootstrap of M minus that fixed peer. (This favours the
  peer in every band equally.) Δ vs SSIMULACRA2 is always reported alongside.
- A band shows a **deficit** if the Δ-vs-best-peer 95 % CI lies entirely below 0.
- **Independent units:** CID22-A, CSIQ, KonJND-404, and the JPEG-AIC family counted once
  (the family shows an NT deficit if any member's NT band does).
- T = units with an NT deficit; L = units with a deficit in any MID or LOW band (any member,
  for the family).
- **Confirmed:** |T| ≥ 2 and |L| = 0.
- **Refuted:** |L| ≥ 1 and |L| ≥ |T| (deficits as common outside the top band as in it, or
  only outside it).
- **Unresolved:** everything else, including "no deficit anywhere"; the record names the
  data that would resolve it.
- Reported separately for **B** (served default) and for the **best zensim candidate**,
  fixed now as **C = W10L9PH_s4004** (profile C; highest CID22-49 pooled SROCC in the
  registered ssim2 bar, 2026-08-31). R915 rich, R915 fast, D and PreviewV0_2 are evaluated
  under the same rule and reported, without changing the headline choice.
- Units where M has no rows are MISSING for M and do not count either way.

Sensitivity (reported, not decisive): the same rule with the band SROCC as the statistic;
the family counted per member; best peer fixed per corpus (by global SROCC).
