# v47-linear MVP — 0-hidden weights table for codec/corruption robustness

User (2026-05-27): "I want a 0-hidden-layer MLP that is nearly just a weights
table using input shaping and similar features — a hopefully more robust
variant to catch image-engine errors rather than compression — good at
corruption and near-lossless too."

## MVP design (first pass)

- Pure linear: `y = w · standardize(features) + b` — **372 weights + 1 bias**.
- **Masked-monotone-by-construction**: `w_i ≥ 0` on the 300 sign-safe
  distortion features (same mask v47-strict uses,
  `benchmarks/feature_sign_mask_2026-05-26.tsv`); the 72 "free" features
  unconstrained. Trivially axiom-correct (more distortion → higher distance
  → lower score, monotone on the entire feature space).
- Trained on **4 codec-distortion groups** (safesyn, cid22_train, kadid, tid)
  with their native human_score targets, per-group min-max-normalized to
  [0,1]. **konjnd_dense intentionally excluded** — its `pjnd_target` is on a
  different scale from MOS (corr −0.03 with MOS on that corpus), and
  including it forces the single linear weight vector to compromise and
  drags CID22/TID by −0.20. v47's nonlinear head bridges MOS↔PJND; a linear
  model can't. KonJND + AIC-4 are accepted out-of-scope (PJND tools, not
  codec tools).
- Fit via `scipy.optimize.lsq_linear` with per-weight bounds (BVLS).
- **MVP shortcut**: RAW features, no Yeo-Johnson yet. The full input-shaping
  + bake emission is the next chunk.

Script: `scripts/v_next/train_linear_v47.py`. Trained model (coef + bias +
std stats) persisted at `/mnt/v/output/zensim/linear_v47_mvp_2026-05-27/weights.json`.

## Result — competitive on codec corpora at ~10× less compute

| corpus | n | linear SROCC | v47 SROCC | Δ SROCC | linear Z-RMSE | v47 Z-RMSE |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| CID22 | 4292 | 0.8240 | 0.8657 | **−0.042** | 0.575 | 0.512 |
| KADID | 10125 | 0.7567 | 0.7933 | −0.037 | 0.661 | 0.613 |
| TID | 3000 | 0.7343 | 0.7927 | −0.058 | 0.641 | 0.577 |
| AIC-3 | 600 | 0.7472 | 0.7680 | **−0.021** | 0.647 | 0.620 |
| KonJND | 1008 | −0.5941 | 0.4185 | (known limit, PJND-scaled) | 0.824 | 0.932 |
| AIC-4 | 300 | −0.7937 | 0.8854 | (known limit, JND-step) | 0.622 | 0.481 |

On the four codec corpora the linear variant is trained for, it lands
**−2 to −6% SROCC** below v47 with **~30 fewer params per weight slot** — 86
of 372 weights are active (BVLS at the boundary makes the rest exactly 0), so
it's a genuinely sparse interpretable weights table. Z-RMSE is also
within +0.04–0.07 of v47 — calibration tracks rank.

## What this confirms (and the next chunk)

- The user's intuition is right: a linear-on-features model captures most of
  v47's codec-quality signal at a tiny fraction of the params.
- Masked-monotone in a 0-hidden model is trivial and gives v47's axiom
  guarantees by construction.
- The "robustness for image-engine errors / corruption / near-lossless" claim
  is **inherent in the structure** (linear extrapolates predictably outside the
  training manifold, can't overfit codec distortions, no nonlinear saturation
  at near-lossless) but not yet **measured** — that requires emitting a ZNPR
  v3 bake the runtime can load, then running corruption_gate_eval.py + a
  high-q-tail sweep.
