## Result: low aggregate activation, concentrated low-quality failures

The unchanged head activates on **26/8,880 product reconstructions (0.293%)**
and **0/264 distinct native JXL interventions** (272 attempts). Combined,
26/9,144 nonidentity pairs activate (0.284%). There are another 1,619 identity
pairs with zero activations; they are excluded from that denominator.
The complete packet contains 10,771 attempts, 10,763 distinct pairs, 148 origins
and 141 source families. Product data cover 19 content classes; native records
retain four shorter class aliases rather than silently merging classifications.

This does not qualify the head. Some sources were used by its historical fit

Activation concentrates at the lowest stored generic quality: AVIF q5 is
10/296 (3.38%) and JPEG q5 is 14/296 (4.73%). AVIF also has one activation at
q15 and one at q85; other recorded groups have zero. These are descriptive

`worst_stored_native` field contains all 8,880 reconstructions and **does not
establish codec floors or a lowest-quality-per-image comparison**.

All 26 active pairs have negative base scores (−86.37 through −34.03).
Consequently, `min(base, corruption)` changes none of their scalar scores.
A separately registered post-score API diagnostic replayed all 26 activations
and all 272 native attempts with prepared-steering checks enabled:
**26/26 return `REJECTED_INTEGRITY`; 272/272 native attempts are accepted**,
with 14,144 map queries exactly matching the base-only map. The scalar result
alone therefore hides a material product failure: an apparently unchanged score
can still prohibit steering. This conditional replay is not another population
activation estimate.
