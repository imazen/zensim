## What it says

- **The served entry at 4K, single thread, is 709 ms** for 16.8 MP — 42 ms
  per megapixel — and 0.53× of `fast_ssim2`'s time on the same core. With
  eight threads on one CCD it is 194 ms; with all sixteen cores 102 ms.
- **The folded 944 extraction does not scale**: 2.2× on eight threads and
  2.5× on sixteen, against 3.5-3.8× / 5.4-6.2× for the buffered v1 path on
  the same box and 3.7× / 7.0× for the served `B` entry. `fold156_basic` is
  flat from eight to sixteen threads (119 → 120 ms). That is where the
  threaded time goes now: the fold walk's serial share — the whole-window
  v2-phase V sweeps and the per-strip retention/attribution copies the
  single-thread profile already names — not the SSIM arithmetic, which
  the same-binary A/B shows is revision-neutral.
- Revision 3 is not in any of these numbers: the default revision ran, and
  the base-vs-current A/B in the fusion record shows the default path
  unchanged by the #61 work.
