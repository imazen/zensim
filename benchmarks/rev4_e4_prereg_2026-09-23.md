## 1. Hypothesis

A candidate's rate of disagreement with the two-reference rule on the TRAIN-side ladder
instrument (SSIMULACRA2 and butteraugli-pnorm3 agree on an order; the candidate reverses it
materially) predicts its held-out human outcome and its dial-contract outcome **beyond what
the registered composite already predicts**. H0: no incremental prediction.

## 2. Program decision rule (copied)

> adopt it as a selection gate only if it predicts held-out outcomes the composite misses,
> with a CI. It never becomes a training signal again without new evidence.

(E4 brief: "Adopt agreement as a selection gate only if it predicts held-out outcomes that the
composite misses, with a CI excluding zero. Otherwise record a negative result.")

**Operationalisation.** ADOPT iff, in the MAIN population, for at least one of the six
primary outcomes (§5) the partial SROCC ρ(−P_dis, Y | C_LOO(Y)) has a family-clustered
bootstrap CI at the Bonferroni level 1 − 0.05/6 (99.17 %, two-sided percentile) that lies
entirely above zero, AND no primary outcome has that CI entirely below zero. Otherwise
NEGATIVE. If ADOPT holds for P_dis but the butteraugli-only arm (P_bu) does not also
exclude zero on the same outcome at 95 %, the verdict is recorded as "ADOPT, circularity
unresolved" (see §8).

## 5. Variables

Predictors (TRAIN-side ladder, 9,411 adjacent pairs, reference `unknown = 0`; lower is better;
correlations use the negation so that a positive SROCC means "predicts a better outcome"):

- **P_dis (primary)** — exact two-reference disagreement: over pairs where both references
  agree materially (forward: Δssim2 ≥ +0.5 AND Δbutteraugli ≤ −0.05; backward: Δssim2 ≤ −0.5
  AND Δbutteraugli ≥ +0.05; 5,232 pairs), the fraction the candidate reverses by > 0.5 dial pt.
- **P_dial** — 1 − `mono_agree` (registered C1 quantity; dial-attributed material inversions).
- **P_bu (circularity sensitivity arm)** — butteraugli-only: over pairs with |Δbutteraugli| ≥
  0.05 (5,619), fraction the candidate reverses materially.
- **P_s2 (exploratory contrast)** — ssim2-only analogue (7,416 pairs).
- **P_dis_nofloor (sensitivity)** — P_dis excluding each ladder's lowest 3 pairs (A7r window),
  to decouple from the floor outcome.

Composite (the control):
- **C_A** — `freeze_check::balanced_composite` registered weights (CID22 1.00, imazen26 0.50,
  nonphoto 0.30, KonJND 0.20, CSIQ 0.15, LIVE 0.15, AIC-3 0.10, AIC-4 0.05; abs SROCC), with the
  CID22 term **re-cut to CID22-A(25)** and the CID22 band-tail term (0.15) **omitted** because it
  is a CID22-49 statistic; absent terms drop from numerator and denominator (owner rule). The
  other terms are the stored `rank.<corpus>.srocc` of the cell's fulleval.
- **C_LOO(Y)** — C_A with outcome Y's own term removed (for the dial outcomes, C_LOO = C_A).
- **Combo** — the rank average of −P_dis and C_LOO(Y) (average ranks for ties).

Outcomes (primary set of six):
- **H1 CID22-A(25)** signed SROCC, candidate scores from `ensemble_score_rows` on A-only rows
  (rows filtered by `ref_basename` membership before any label is touched).
- **H2 AIC-3** stored `rank.aic3.srocc_signed` (quality-oriented).
- **H3 KonJND-504** stored `rank.konjnd`, magnitude reading −`srocc_signed` (distortion-oriented
  per the owner registry); n must be 504.
- **H4 CSIQ** stored `rank.csiq.srocc_signed` (owner orientation).
- **D1 dial contract** — 1 if any MEASURED contract check among C2–C6 is `fail`, else 0
  (C1 excluded: it is P_dial itself). Oriented so higher = better (pass = 1).
- **D2 floors** — mean over the five codecs of A7r `represented_frac` (ssim2-mentor `resolvable`
  rule).

## 6. Statistics

- All SROCCs by `panel` (`zen_stats.panel_batch_indexed`, stats="srocc"); no hand-written SROCC.
- Lineage-clustered bootstrap, **B = 4000, seed 20260923** (`random.Random`): each resample
  draws 19 lineages with replacement and takes every member cell of each drawn lineage. The
  same index set is applied to every predictor/outcome/composite vector in a resample (paired).
- Per outcome Y: ρ(−P, Y) for each predictor, ρ(C_LOO, Y), ρ(Combo, Y); partial
  ρ(−P, Y | C_LOO) = (ρ_PY − ρ_PC ρ_CY)/√((1−ρ_PC²)(1−ρ_CY²)) from the three owner SROCCs of
  the same resample; paired Δ = ρ(Combo, Y) − ρ(C_LOO, Y). 95 % and 99.17 % percentile CIs.
- Every cell reports n (cells) and lineages.
- Per-candidate CID22-A SROCC also gets a reference-clustered CI (25 refs, B = 2000, seed
  20260923) for the record table of named anchors only; not used in the decision.

## 7. Gate view

Threshold fixed here from the predictor distribution alone: **τ = median P_dis over MAIN =
0.009938837920489297** (reject a candidate if P_dis > τ). "Failed" = D1 = 0 (measured contract
failure) OR held-out mean percentile rank over H1–H4 in the bottom tertile of MAIN. Report the
2×2 confusion table (reject/keep × failed/ok), and the same table for a composite gate at the
median of C_A for comparison. Descriptive only; the decision is §2.

## 8. Circularity (stated in advance)

SSIMULACRA2 is a teacher of most candidates and one of the two references; the A7r floor rule
also uses ssim2 as mentor. Agreement with the two-reference rule therefore partly measures
agreement with a teacher. P_bu is the sensitivity arm that removes ssim2 from the predictor;
D2 remains ssim2-shaped and is labelled so. KADID/TID are TRAIN-role and memorised in several
eras; KADID is read only as a root-identity check.

## 9. Data roles and exposure

Reads of held-out human labels (CID22-A(25) via A-only rows; stored AIC-3, KonJND-504, CSIQ

Nothing here feeds a fit, a calibration or a checkpoint choice.
