### 1.2 Why a matched-leg control is REQUIRED, and what it makes the D row mean

`D-id100-negrich` is fit on the **frozen 372-wide safesyn Gram**
(`/mnt/v/output/zensim-multicodec-probe/linear-probe/grams/safesyn.npz`,
196,086 weighted rows). The free set does not exist at 372 width, so no free arm
can be fit on that Gram. Every free arm here is fit on the only leg that carries
the free slots — the **111,068-row pools-944 safesyn table** — which
`benchmarks/free_features_2026-09-01.md` §12.1/§3.3 (inheriting
`hybrid_candidate_2026-09-01.md` §12.1) already measured to cap CID22
**0.05–0.15 below** ADD156's real leg, *on both arms equally*.

**Therefore: an absolute rank number from a free arm is NOT comparable to
`D-id100-negrich`'s published number, and this doc does not present it as one.**
The free-set question is answered by the **within-leg** contrast
(`Ffree` − `Fctl`, `FfreeC` − `Ffree`), where leg, lambda, Gram, anchor, solver
and every flag are held fixed and the coordinate slice is the only thing that
moves. `D-id100-negrich`'s numbers are quoted as the **incumbent**, from
`benchmarks/d_id100_2026-09-04.md`, and are not re-run.

### 1.3 λ — pre-registered at 0.3, with the reason

ADD156's own registered λ is 2e-3, but on this leg λ=2e-3 leaves 151/156
coefficients active — a dense model, not ADD156's 28-coefficient regime.
`benchmarks/hybrid_candidate_2026-09-01.md` §11.2 swept λ on this exact leg and
found λ ≈ 0.3 puts the 156-only control at 26 active. The free-features probe
took λ=0.3 as "the operating point that means something" for the same reason.
**This lane fits at λ=0.3 only**, and reuses that lane's λ=0.3 bakes as its base
arms so the base is a byte-reproduced artifact rather than a new fit.

### 1.5 The anchor rule, stated before it was used

- **Source**: `r1b-pools944-2026-08-30/ext_safesyn_full.parquet` (111,068 rows,
  sha `e33ec91e…`), `dial_target = human_score × 100` — the same convention the

  (*"stride 55; target_score = human_score*100 (DIAL units, matching the era-1

- **Stride 55**, minus any row also selected by the negative-tail probe, so
  anchor and probe are **disjoint by construction** (the D lane's §6.2

- **identity ×21** rows appended with `target_score = 100`, giving a 1.05 %
  identity share against the D lane's 1.03 % — the "smallest mass that can
  express identity ↦ 100 and no more" of `d_id100_2026-09-04.md` §5.

## 3. A finding the 372 gate could not have made: at 944 width the identity vector is NOT zero

`dial_addressability.rs` carries a named constant asserting the opposite —
*"ref == dist yields all-zero features for every image; identity dial =
dial(0-vector)"* — and the 372 probe's own registry row records it as a measured
property. **At `folded720append2pools` width it is false.**

MEASURED on the 39-row probe: **286 of 944 slots are non-zero on at least one
row, and they vary by image.** They include the fold's own basic block
(`f0`, `f1`, `f2`: 4.7e-6 … 8.1e-4 across the 39 references), every peak triple,
the whole `PJND_FRAGILITY` family (a formula artifact
`free_features_classC_2026-09-04.md` §6.3 already documents), and
`LUMA_MEAN_REF`, which is *reference-only* and therefore cannot be zero on a
ref==dist pair by construction.

Two consequences the gate already handles correctly, and one it does not:

- **C5 still works**, because `IdentityMeasure` counts rows outside the band
  rather than testing one scalar — this lane's arms read `dial_min` 99.7893 …
  `dial_max` 100.0000 over 39 rows, 0 outside.
- **C6 still works**, because `n_above_identity` compares each grid cell against
  `dial_max`, the most permissive identity value.
- **The `note` string is wrong at this width** and is emitted verbatim into every
  `--gaddr-json` and into the registry row. It is registered in the new identity
  row's `measured_property` field as a correction rather than propagated; fixing
  the constant to be conditional on the probe is registered, not done here.

The peer's own reading is unaffected: ssim2 identity is **100.000000** on all 39
(38 from the 372 lane's `zenmetrics batch --metric ssim2` run, the 39th —
`9059ec43b26aa167_769x513`, present on the 944 grids but not the 372 one —
measured here by the same tool).

**Round-trip control on the three new registry rows:** grading `peer_ssim2`
against its own freshly-appended pins reads **SHIPPABLE — 15 pass / 0 fail /
0 not-measured**, every bar tied bit-exactly. Independently, the four grid
scalars the parked `gaddrinst` lane had computed for the full 944 grid by a
separate route (`min −55.354544, max 99.993468, p5 12.0158692, p95 95.4858354`)
are reproduced here **exactly** through the owner.

## 6. The result the lane did not go looking for: the ZERO-COST half carries the gain

The 109-slot free set has two halves with very different prices
(`free_features_2026-09-01.md` §4):

- **72 peaks (`f156..227`)** — already computed unconditionally by
  `fused_vblur_ssim_inner*` and shipped by `V1PoolsMode::Peaks`; the only cost is
  emitting them into a wider vector, and that lane's §2.1 measured it at CI's
  straddling 1.0 on every one of nine size × thread cells.
- **37 raw-moment slots** — need `V1FreeExtras::RawMoments`, priced honestly at
  **+0.8 % … +1.6 % of the 156 walk at 1T**, and carrying a **9.12 % real-pixel
  route-parity miss** on `GLOBAL_CGAIN`/`GLOBAL_CLOSS`
  (`free_features_classC_2026-09-04.md` §6.2).

`Fpeaks` was added to this lane's arm set (post-registration; disclosed as such)
to split them. **It gets essentially the whole gain:**

| axis | `Ffree` − `Fctl` | `Fpeaks` − `Fctl` | fraction of the gain the peaks alone deliver |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| cid22 | +0.04054 | +0.03947 | **97 %** |
| konjnd | +0.08134 | +0.07732 | **95 %** |

| `hfnl_cid22band` pooled | +0.0527 | +0.0504 | **96 %** |
| `hfnl_cid22band` per-ref | +0.0291 | +0.0284 | **98 %** |

…and on the remaining eight corpora `Fpeaks` is **better** than `Ffree`, not
worse — kadid **+0.00801** [+0.00713, +0.00896], csiq **+0.00466**, tid
**+0.00475**, imazen26 **+0.00173**, nonphoto **+0.00169**, aic4 **+0.00130**,

### 8.1 The reading this lane REJECTS, and why it is being told anyway

Those same runs make `free156_peaks_raw` look **3.6 % faster than
`peaks156_no_raw`** (and the `*_extract_only` diagnostic reproduces it in the
WALK, −4.3 %), which is physically backwards: `15f`'s compute set is a strict
superset of `15c`'s. Rather than publish it, the pair was re-measured with the
**owner's own instrument** — `scripts/freefeats_ab.sh` driving
`zensim/examples/foldapp_stream_bigpair`, i.e. one binary, arms at runtime,
byte-identical 3-character env values, CCD0-pinned, min of 7 inner walks × 15
process starts, which is the era-2 §22.5 protocol the free-features lane's own
numbers were produced under:

| | `15c` (peaks) | `15f` (peaks + raw moments) | `15f`/`15c` |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| min over 15 starts | 107.440 ms | 107.660 ms | **1.0020 (+0.20 %)** |
| median over 15 starts | 108.410 ms | 108.280 ms | 0.9988 (−0.12 %) |

**So the inversion is an instrument artifact, not a property of the walks.** At
2304²/1T on this box today the raw-moment marginal is inside the noise floor;
the free-features lane's 15-start sweep of 2026-09-01 measured +0.8 – +1.6 % at
1T across three sizes. Both readings are small and neither is negative. The
`ssim2_speed_bar` arms disagree because that harness gave only **5 rounds per
arm** at this size (CV up to 45 % in the 576² cell) on a box carrying other
lanes — exactly the failure mode `profile_d_notax_2026-09-01.md` §4 registered,
where a tight-budget zenbench group can report a *spuriously low* mean that
`min()` then happily selects.

**Consequence for §6: the peaks-only recommendation rests on RANK, not on
speed.** The honest speed statement is that the raw-moment half costs
**0 – 1.6 %** of the walk — cheap, not free — and buys nothing at this operating
point.

**W1/W2/W3/W5/W6/W7** are unchanged from the ADD156 column of the exam and are
not re-graded here: every arm is a 944-wide additive head on a leg that
§1.2 already prices below ADD156's, so a W1/W2 re-grade off this leg would
measure the leg, not the model.

## 9. The answer, and what it does and does not license

**The question — "did the +free get tried for D-id100-negrich or just the base

**Does the free set improve the D-id100-negrich recipe?** On the matched leg,
holding Gram, λ, anchor, solver and every flag fixed, **yes on the axes the
product cares most about and no on three**:

| verdict | axes |
|---|---|

**Class-C** is a second, smaller, mostly-different win: **+0.0022 cid22**,

### 9.0 M3a is NOT MEASURED for these arms — deliberately, and it must not be carried

`m3_coherence` / `m3a_coherence` are explicit JSON **null** on all twelve board cells (the
dashboard renders null as an em-dash; a 0.0 would read as "measured, and terrible"). Nothing
in this lane ran `diffmap_block_coherence`, so nothing is claimed.

**Do not fill them by carrying a value from a sibling bake.** MEASURED by the replication
lane (coordinator, 2026-09-05): **M3a is PACK-sensitive** — the same recipe and the same
seed, packed twice, reads **0.7489 vs 0.8618**. So an M3a number is a property of *a
specific packed artifact*, not of a recipe, and the E.4 tie-break contribution it feeds
(`balanced_composite + 0.15·M3a`) is likewise pack-scoped. Every arm here is an *unpacked*
`fit-lasso` output (no `bake_dial_refit pack` step — a sparse additive head takes
`--zerobias-bulk 0` and would be rank-exact, but that pass was not run). If any arm is
later packed, its M3a must be **re-measured on the packed bytes**, never inherited from the
unpacked fit or from `Fctl`/`Ffree`/`FfreeC` sharing a recipe family.

### 9.1 What is NOT claimed

- **No comparison to `D-id100-negrich`'s published numbers.** They are on a
  different training leg (§1.2). The measured size of that gap here is the
  matched control's own CID22, **0.80713 against D's 0.86338 — −0.056**, right
  inside the 0.05–0.15 the earlier lanes priced. Every free-arm number in this
  doc is therefore a *within-leg* result.
- **No arm beats ssim2** on any axis measured here, `hfnl_cid22band` included
  (P = 0.000 throughout). Closing that is a leg question, not a feature question.
- **W1/W2/W3 are not re-graded** (§8).
- **A7/A9 are not addressed.** They are the D lane's structural pair.

### 9.2 Ship proposal — PROPOSAL ONLY, nothing is flipped

If the D lineage is to adopt anything from this lane, the recommendation is
**`Fpeaks`'s slice, not `Ffree`'s**: `scripts/sota944/slice_basic156_peaks.txt`
(228 coordinates = the 156 basic slots + the 72 peaks). What it would change
relative to a 156-only D-class model *on the same leg*:

- **+0.039 CID22, +0.077 KonJND, +0.070 hfnlproxy, +0.043 kadid, +0.034 CSIQ,
  +0.015 LIVE**, and **+0.050 pooled / +0.028 per-ref** on `hfnl_cid22band` —
  the near-lossless zone the metric is documented weakest in.
- **−0.054 SDR25, −0.022 AIC-4, −0.017 imazen26, −0.017 nonphoto.** The last two
  are ssim2 self-targets, so they measure *agreement with ssim2*, not a human
  loss; SDR25 (n=50) and AIC-4 (n=300) are genuine human corpora and the cost
  there is real.
- **Cost: none that has been measured.** The peaks are already computed; only the
  output vector widens, and that lane's nine-cell sweep put every ratio CI across
  1.0. It needs `V1PoolsMode::Peaks` and a `folded720append2pools`-class table,
  both of which ship today.
- **No exposure to the free-40 route-parity skew** (§7), which `Ffree` has at
  0.55 % of its contribution mass.
- **Dial: identical treatment.** `Fpeaks-id100-negrich` is CONTRACT 6/6,
  REGRESSION 7/9, identity 99.7907…100.0000 over 39 references, 0 of 4,817 grid
  cells above identity.

The cost of adopting `Ffree` on top of `Fpeaks` is ~1 % of walk time, the skew
exposure, and a *net-negative* rank trade on 8 of 12 corpora. The cost of
adopting `FfreeC` on top is a further ~1.3–1.5 % (native tier) for a real
mid-range gain and no near-lossless gain.

**Neither is proposed for a default.** Both are proposed as a slice choice for
whoever next fits a D-class model on a 944-width leg — and the prerequisite for
making any of it comparable to the shipped Profile D remains the one
`free_features_2026-09-01.md` §3.3 already named: a pools-944 re-extraction of
ADD156's own 196k safesyn leg.
