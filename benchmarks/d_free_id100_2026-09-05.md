### 1.2 Why a matched-leg control is REQUIRED, and what it makes the D row mean

`D-id100-negrich` is fit on the **frozen 372-wide safesyn Gram**
(`/mnt/v/output/zensim-multicodec-probe/linear-probe/grams/safesyn.npz`,
196,086 weighted rows). The free set does not exist at 372 width, so no free arm
can be fit on that Gram. Every free arm here is fit on the only leg that carries
the free slots — the **111,068-row pools-944 safesyn table** — which
`benchmarks/free_features_2026-09-01.md` §12.1/§3.3 (inheriting
`hybrid_candidate_2026-09-01.md` §12.1) already measured to cap CID22
**0.05–0.15 below** ADD156's real leg, *on both arms equally*.

**Therefore: an absolute rank number from a free arm is NOT comparable to
`D-id100-negrich`'s published number, and this doc does not present it as one.**
The free-set question is answered by the **within-leg** contrast
(`Ffree` − `Fctl`, `FfreeC` − `Ffree`), where leg, lambda, Gram, anchor, solver
and every flag are held fixed and the coordinate slice is the only thing that
moves. `D-id100-negrich`'s numbers are quoted as the **incumbent**, from
`benchmarks/d_id100_2026-09-04.md`, and are not re-run.

### 1.3 λ — pre-registered at 0.3, with the reason

ADD156's own registered λ is 2e-3, but on this leg λ=2e-3 leaves 151/156
coefficients active — a dense model, not ADD156's 28-coefficient regime.
`benchmarks/hybrid_candidate_2026-09-01.md` §11.2 swept λ on this exact leg and
found λ ≈ 0.3 puts the 156-only control at 26 active. The free-features probe
took λ=0.3 as "the operating point that means something" for the same reason.
**This lane fits at λ=0.3 only**, and reuses that lane's λ=0.3 bakes as its base
arms so the base is a byte-reproduced artifact rather than a new fit.

### 1.5 The anchor rule, stated before it was used

- **Source**: `r1b-pools944-2026-08-30/ext_safesyn_full.parquet` (111,068 rows,
  sha `e33ec91e…`), `dial_target = human_score × 100` — the same convention the
  era-2 `anchor944_pools_dial.parquet` manifest states
  (*"stride 55; target_score = human_score*100 (DIAL units, matching the era-1
  multiband_anchor_dial100 convention)"*). Rule reused, not invented.
- **Stride 55**, minus any row also selected by the negative-tail probe, so
  anchor and probe are **disjoint by construction** (the D lane's §6.2
  discipline). 2,020 stride rows − 44 collisions = **1,976**.
- **clamped** variant `max(dial_target, 0)` — the era-1
  `target_score = max(ssim2_gpu, 0)` convention → the `-id100` arms.
- **negrich** variant unclamped — 75 genuinely negative rows to −62.17 → the
  `-id100-negrich` arms. (The D lane's own anchor carries 147 negatives to
  −64.16: same shape.)
- **identity ×21** rows appended with `target_score = 100`, giving a 1.05 %
  identity share against the D lane's 1.03 % — the "smallest mass that can
  express identity ↦ 100 and no more" of `d_id100_2026-09-04.md` §5.

### 1.6 Pre-registered success criterion

An arm **wins** if, on the matched leg, it (a) holds CONTRACT 6/6, and (b) does
not regress the genuine human-referenced holdouts (`cid22`, `konjnd`, `aic3`,
`csiq`, `live`, `sdr25`, `aic4`) against `Fctl-id100-negrich` outside a paired
bootstrap CI. The free set's registered expectation
(`free_features_2026-09-01.md` §3.1/§3.2) is CID22 **+0.04**, KonJND **+0.08**,
`hfnl_cid22band` **+0.05 pooled**, against a real **−0.06 SDR25** cost — those
are the numbers this lane is testing, not assuming.

### 1.7 Carried forward: the free-40 precision skew

`free_features_classC_2026-09-04.md` §6.2 measured that **9.12 % of the free-40
raw-moment cells** miss their own 2e-5 route-parity bar on real corpus pixels
(`GLOBAL_CLOSS` 1,467 · `GLOBAL_CGAIN` 1,132 · `GLOBAL_DMEAN` 8 ·
`LUMA_MEAN_REF` 0), worst |Δ| **3.63e-3**, growing with plane size, mechanism =
catastrophic cancellation staged differently by the two routes. **Any arm here
that spends coefficients on `GLOBAL_CGAIN`/`GLOBAL_CLOSS` is trained on one
route's values and would be served the other's.** This is registered, not
hidden; the per-arm coefficient census in §4 reports exactly how much weight
each arm puts on the affected slots.
