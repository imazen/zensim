## Motivation

After the dHash contamination audit was retracted as fundamentally
flawed (commit `a586a41`), the natural question was: did the
2026-05-12 purge that removed 361 training sources (~73k pairs) hurt
V0_18? The 218k features CSV at
`/tmp/zensim_loop/safe_synth_218k_features.csv` was still on disk.

## Interpretation

Counter to the expectation that restoring "false-positive purged"
sources would improve V0_18:

1. The 2026-05-12 dHash purge was based on a flawed audit (loose
   d ≤ 16 threshold). User-verified review proved the 361 flagged
   sources are NOT visual duplicates of CID22 refs.
2. Adding those 361 sources back into training (V0_18.1) reduces
   CID22 SROCC by 0.011 aggregate, with the largest hit at B2.
3. Plausible explanations:
   - **Content distribution shift**: the 361 sources happen to be
     content-shifted away from the CID22 sub-distribution (different
     image categories, color statistics, edge density, etc) and
     pull the MLP's capacity away from CID22-like content.
   - **Limited capacity**: the 228 → 128 (per component) → 384 (concat) → 1
     architecture is capacity-constrained; more diverse training data
     spreads supervision thinner per CID22-like example.
   - **Random regression to the V0_2 corpus mean**: V0_2 was trained
     on this exact 218k corpus and achieved CID22 0.8676 — V0_18.1's
     0.8825 is closer to V0_2's number than V0_18's 0.8934.
4. The 0.011 difference is not an artifact — KADID/TID **improve**
   by 0.003-0.004 with the same retrain, so the training pipeline is
   sound. It's a real corpus-shape effect.

## Implication for future work

The intuition that "more data is always better" doesn't hold when
the marginal data shifts content distribution away from the target
holdout (CID22). For V0_20+ work the right move is **not just more
data** but **better-matched data** — and the principled answer per
the literature notes is:

- **distortion-manifold pre-training** (Su 2023, task #39): learn
  content-invariant distortion representation from unlabeled pairs;
  fit head on small labeled set. Should immunize the model from
  corpus-shape mismatches.
- **JPEG-AI training corpus acquisition**: directly extends the
  training distribution into B0/B1.
