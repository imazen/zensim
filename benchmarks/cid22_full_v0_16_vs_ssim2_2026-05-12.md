# CID22 (full paired-comparison, n=4292) — V0_16 vs fast-ssim2 vs dssim

**First measurement** of V0_16 against the **full 4292-row CID22
paired-comparison set** (MCOS scale, all 9 codecs × ~50 settings ×
~10 q levels). Previously the ship benchmark was on a smaller 49-ref
validation subset.

## Aggregate |SROCC| vs MCOS (n=4292)

| Metric | \|SROCC\| | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| **fast-ssim2-gpu** | **0.8894** | canonical reference |
| dssim-gpu          | 0.8722 | -0.0172 vs ssim2 |
| **zensim V0_16**   | **0.8674** | **-0.0220 vs ssim2** |
| bpp                | 0.6277 | rate-quality baseline |

**V0_16 falls 0.022 SROCC short of fast-ssim2** on the full CID22.
Goal #1 from CLAUDE.md ("match-or-exceed fast-ssim2 across all
quality bands") is **not empirically met** at this evaluation scale.

Note: ship docs quote V0_16 CID22 SROCC = 0.8919 (above fast-ssim2's
0.8895). That number is from a DIFFERENT evaluation: the 49-ref
validation subset using `dataset_metric_baseline` harness (which
applies affine calibration + score mapping). `zen-metrics batch`
hits the raw MLP output on the full 4292-pair set. Both are valid
measurements; they differ on what they measure. The comparison-site
shows the FULL set number (honest, complete-data answer).

## Cross-corpus reconciliation

Three distinct cross-corpus pictures of V0_16 vs the
quality-metric field:

| Corpus | n | V0_16 | fast-ssim2 | dssim | V0_16 vs ssim2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AIC-3 CTC EPFL | 600  | **0.7962** | 0.7970 | 0.7884 | -0.0008 (tie) |
| AIC-4 sample   | 300  | 0.9107 | 0.9127 | 0.9256 | -0.0020 (tie) |
| **CID22 (full)** | **4292** | **0.8674** | **0.8894** | 0.8722 | **-0.0220 (LOSS)** |

**The CID22 result is the outlier**. AIC-3 and AIC-4 both show V0_16
within ±0.002 of fast-ssim2 (the goal #1 bar). CID22 — the canonical
gold-standard corpus that goal #1 specifically calls out as the
shipping gate — shows V0_16 trailing by 0.022.

## Cycle-7 implications

The full-CID22 result reinforces three cycle-7 directions:

1. **Densify AVIF/HEIC distortion samples in synth corpus.** V0_16's
   biggest losses (AVIF_aurora_fast -0.063, AVIF_aom_s7 -0.025,
   AVIF_aom_s1 -0.022) are all on modern AV1-derived encoders that
   the V_X training distribution under-covers.
2. **Investigate the 49-ref vs 4292-pair discrepancy.** The 0.022
   ship-doc/full-set gap is large enough that it's not just noise.
   The 49-ref V0_16 = 0.8919 may reflect a sweet-spot subset where
   V0_16 was implicitly optimized; the 4292-pair number is the
   honest generalization performance.
3. **Consider dssim as a co-training signal.** Aggregate-wise dssim
   sits between V0_16 and fast-ssim2 on CID22, but on AIC-4 dssim
   outperformed both. The pattern suggests dssim captures something
   V0_X currently misses; adding it to the trainer's loss as an
   auxiliary head could lift V0_X without compromising the
   ssim2-correlation objective.

## Reproducibility

```bash
# Score CID22 with V0_16 + dssim + fast-ssim2:
zen-metrics batch --metric zensim       --pairs pairs.tsv --output zensim.tsv
zen-metrics batch --metric dssim-gpu    --pairs pairs.tsv --output dssim.tsv
zen-metrics batch --metric ssim2-gpu    --pairs pairs.tsv --output ssim2.tsv

python3 export_human_corpora_to_parquet.py --which cid22 \
  --metrics-tsv zensim.tsv \
  --metrics-tsv dssim.tsv \
  --metrics-tsv ssim2.tsv \
  --out cid22.parquet

python3 -c "
import pyarrow.parquet as pq
from scipy.stats import spearmanr
df = pq.read_table('cid22.parquet').to_pandas()
sub = df[df['codec'] != 'Reference']
for col in ['score_ssim2_gpu', 'score_zensim', 'score_dssim']:
    rho, _ = spearmanr(sub[col], sub['human_mos'])
    print(f'{col}: |SROCC|={abs(rho):.4f}')
"
```
