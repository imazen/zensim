# V2SPATIAL — why supported v2 terms mis-ranked block repairs, and the fix that landed

2026-10-02 (Mountain Time), lane `steercheck`, follow-up to `benchmarks/steercheck_2026-10-02.md`. Rev3 tables and bakes only.
Development evidence on TRAIN-role pairs (broad panel) and the 4 eval-source owner cases; nothing here qualifies a model.

## Result in one paragraph

Two separate causes. (1) A **numerical bug**, now fixed: in the fused attribution pass B the scalar tail (the last `width % 8` columns of
every pyramid-scale plane) computed the v2 SSIM dissimilarity `d` with the covariance form, but at revision 3 that plane holds the direct
error moment. `d` left [0, 2] (hundreds), and the SSIM-deviation coefficients (`1/(4 f4^3)`, up to 1e14 on near-lossless coarse scales)
turned it into map values of 1e16-1e27 in the affected blocks, which destroyed the rank. (2) A **structural limit, not fixed**: the
`BLOCKINESS` map splits each lattice step 50/50 between its two pixels, which is not what a block repair does to a step that straddles the
repaired rectangle. With (1) fixed it is the only family whose oracle substitution moves rank.

Other candidates, same 96 cases (before -> after passes): v2-only 24 -> 47, v2+basic minus BANDING/FRAGILITY 27 -> 46, full944 and
masked/IW sets stay 96/96 UNSUPPORTED. **Controls are bit-identical** (basic156 83, basic228 86, local120 78, y60 92: M2 and M3f
differ by 0.0 on all 96 cases each). The base scores of all 1,056 rows are identical before and after (max |diff| 0.0). The owner's 12
eval-source checks (KADID, widths divisible by 8 at every scale, so no tail columns) do not change: v2+basic stays 2/12.
Still failing after the fix: 33 of 96 (26 on M3f, 7 on M2): 12 graphic, 10 screen, 7 document, 4 photo.

## 1. Localization

Tool: the existing `diffmap_block_coherence`, extended in place (`ZENSIM_V2_DIAG=<path>`, example only, no library API) to write, for every
feature ID with nonzero sensitivity, (a) the predicted per-block map sum of that ID alone through the attribution owner (buffered and fused
entries) and (b) the observed `s_k * dF_k` from the finite reference-block repair. Run on the 24-pair register, block 32, with the
uniform three-seed v2basic ensemble. `v2diag.py`, `v2an2.py` here; per-case JSON (700 MB) is in
`~/tmp/zensim-paper/rev4/steercheck-artifacts/v2spatial/` (not committed).

* **Before the fix**, 9 of 24 cases had a served-map block 1e16-1e26 while the per-ID buffered (f64) sums were <= 2: the f32 fused path
  disagreed with the f64 path on ID 664 (SSIM_DEV4, scale 3, Y) by 3.4e26. Same coefficient in both paths (`k4 = 1.1e14`, f4 = 2.3e-6, printed
  from `derive_v2app_coeffs`), so the difference was in the per-pixel `d`. The offending blocks sit over the tail columns (image 157 is
  171 px wide: scale-3 plane width 22, tail from column 16 = source x 128, block 4).
* **After the fix** per-ID fused and buffered sums agree (max |F-P| 1.1e-4 on image 157, additivity 2e-6) and the 24-case block-32 median
  M3f is 0.827 (20 of 24 pass M3f), from the same ensemble.
* **Oracle substitution** (replace one family's predicted map by its observed per-block contribution, scalar model unchanged), after the
  fix, block 32, 24 cases: served 0.827 median / 20 pass; **BLOCKINESS 0.931 / 24 pass**; SSIM dev pools 0.838 / 19; SSIM mean+soft peaks
  0.833 / 20; HF 0.843 / 21; ART/DET/MSE/PJND 0.838 / 21; GMS/RINGING/BANDING/EDGE_WIDTH 0.830 / 20; all v2 observed 0.985; everything 0.999
  (= M2). Dropping the BLOCKINESS prediction instead costs 0.828 -> 0.796 median, so the term helps but is inexact.
* **BLOCKINESS error** (after the fix): the predicted/observed slope is 0.37-0.41 at blocks 8-32 and 0.62 at 64; about 31-32% of blocks have the
  wrong sign for the blockiness part; the L1 error is concentrated at pyramid scales 1-2 (block 32: scale 2 holds 57% of it; block 8: scales 1-2
  hold 73%). Oracle gain shrinks with block size (median M3f 0.605 -> 0.853 at block 8, 0.908 -> 0.962 at block 64).

## 2. Integrand reading

* Fixed site: `attr_pass_b_main_px` (`feature_v2.rs`), the scalar tail of the fused kernel. The vector kernel calls `ssim_d_local_v(..., direct)`
  and the f64 reference `attr_pass_b_for_scale` reads `direct`; the tail helper did not. At Rev3/Rev4 the `s12` plane carries `E[(a-b)^2]`,
  so `cov = s12 - m1*m2` is wrong and `1 - a*c/(b*d)` is unbounded. Rev3+ is where `direct` is on (`active_revision() >= Rev3`).
* Remaining: `attr_pass_b_blockiness(_f32)` gives each lattice step `c_blockiness * bounded_excess(step_dst, step_src, C_BLOCK)` and adds half to
  each of its two pixels ("a step dies only when BOTH pixels are refined"). The feature is the mean over lattice steps of that excess. A
  rectangle whose edge lies on a lattice line (every block size here, 8/16/32/64, aligns with the 8-pixel lattice at scale 0, and at scale s the
  lattice is 8*2^s source pixels) straddles the steps along that edge. After a one-sided repair the step becomes `|s_p - d_q|` (or the mirror),
  whose excess over `|s_p - s_q|` is generally neither 0 nor half of the original: it depends on the signs of the two pixel errors.
  `bounded_excess` is `max(0,.)/(.)`, so the response is piecewise linear and not additive in the two pixels. That matches the observations
  above (wrong-sign blocks, error growing as blocks get smaller and have proportionally more boundary). This is the diagnosis; the
  step-level proof (exact one-sided excess against the observed change) was NOT run, so treat it as supported, not proven.
* Other families have small residual error by the oracle test; no other family's substitution changes pass counts by more than 1 of 24.

## 3. Fix status

* **Landed (maps only, no feature value or served score touched):** `ssim_d_px` + the `direct` flag threaded into `attr_pass_b_main_px`.
  Unit test `feature_v2::tests::pass_b_tail_ssim_d_follows_the_direct_error_moment_form` (4,000 random windows, direct and covariance forms
  against the f64 `ssim_d_local`). Whole library suite with `--features custom-profiles,feature-regime-v2`: 511 passed, 0 failed, 9 ignored (the
  ignored are manual probes and corpus gates, unchanged). Clippy clean on the touched targets; `cargo fmt --check` clean. I did not
  run the negative control (the new test against the old code).
* **Not landed, candidate: exact one-sided blockiness terms.** For each lattice step store `g_p = E - E(p repaired only)`, `g_q = E - E(q repaired
  only)` and the pair term `I = E - g_p - g_q`; give p `g_p + I/2` and q `g_q + I/2` in the density, and have a rectangle query subtract `I/2`
  for each lattice step its edge cuts (a 1-D prefix sum along each lattice line). It is exact for rectangle repairs of this feature because
  blockiness uses raw pixel steps (no blur coupling). Expected effect: the BLOCKINESS oracle above (median M3f 0.83 -> about 0.93, 24 of 24
  block-32 cases over the bar; at block 8, 0.61 -> 0.85). Cost: new query machinery in `AttributionResult` for edge-line terms at each
  pyramid scale plus the bin mapping at coarse scales (a rectangle edge not on a plane lattice line cuts no step; one that is cuts a
  sum-preserved fraction), in both the f64 and f32 passes; I judged that too large to land without your call, and it is not a one-line fix.
* Cheaper partial alternatives (not measured, not recommended): scaling the blockiness map by the observed 0.4 slope (not exact, content
  dependent: 0.37-1.7 across scales), or dropping the term (measured worse, 0.796).

## 4. Rev4 (featpot v2c bakes)

The tail bug is in code that Rev4 shares: `direct` is on for `>= Rev3`, so a Rev4 bake would hit the same fused kernel once serving exists, and
the fix applies to it. The BLOCKINESS limit is a property of the feature's definition (max-type excess over lattice steps), not of the
arithmetic era, so it would apply unchanged to Rev4 v2c bakes. Rev4 serving is still refused (`refuse_rev4_served`); nothing here builds it, and
the Rev4 attribution leaves (`attr_pass_b_*`) are tier-dispatched, so a Rev4 steering measurement is not possible today. Rev4 v2 numerics were
not examined.
