# V0_7 post-fill 4-metric comparison

V0_7 = V0_6 dct_hf retrained on the extended dataset (218k base + ~140k
zenjpeg-420-e1 fills covering SSIM2 0-90), with `--mlp-low-band-oversample
0.5` so half of every batch comes from the SSIM2 [0, 60] band.

V0_7-control is the same training data and architecture without sampler
bias — isolates the data fill from the bias change.

## Per-(metric, dataset) |SROCC| vs human-MOS

| metric | KADIK10k | TID2013 | CID22 |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| V0_7 dct_hf + low-band-bias 0.5 (extended dataset) | 0.8374 | 0.8368 | 0.8906 |
| V0_7-control dct_hf no-bias (extended dataset) | 0.8478 | 0.8268 | 0.8909 |

| V0_5 (original 218k) | 0.8505 | 0.8492 | 0.8934 |

| ref SSIMULACRA 2 | 0.8155 | 0.8525 | 0.8928 |

## V0_7 vs V0_6 dct_hf delta (human-MOS)

| dataset | V0_6 | V0_7 | Δ |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| KADIK10k | 0.8496 | 0.8374 | -0.0122 |
| TID2013 | 0.8416 | 0.8368 | -0.0048 |
| CID22 | 0.8935 | 0.8906 | -0.0029 |

Positive Δ = the e1 fill + sampler bias improved KADID/TID/CID22.
Negative Δ would indicate over-fitting to the new low-q pairs at the
expense of upper-band performance — re-evaluate sampler bias fraction.
