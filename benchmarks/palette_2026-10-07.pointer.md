## Implementation and revision

The design was committed before code (`8a4f8314`). The crate-private research
owner samples at most 32×32 pixel centres, converts to OKLab, sorts/collapses
samples and constructs deterministic weighted median-cut palettes for N=2..8.
Every sample belongs to a cluster; these are representative colours rather
than the N most frequent exact RGB values. An exact subset-DP weighted
assignment matches centres; residual min-cost flow computes population EMD.
Each N emits weighted mean centre shift, signed lightness/chroma/hue change,
population EMD and maximum matched centre shift: 42 slots, f1825..f1866.
Signed lightness/chroma subtract each palette's own weighted mean; hue is a
chroma-weighted matched angular shift. Positions are discarded after sampling.

`palette@w1867/palette_v2#30b09cd1` is available to research extraction and
`zensim-extract-features --palette-only`. Serving bake reads are refused for
all 42 IDs at every formula revision. Inherited family arithmetic is unchanged.
The initial palette_v1 signed-population bug was caught by a stricter global
RGB8 darkening test and fixed at `f9a38a339d8d4ec81e9c99a9587e4762a29c46b2`.
The v1 directory is preserved as superseded evidence; its columns cannot be
mixed with v2 or repaired by flipping cached signs.

Owners: [kernel](../zensim/src/palette.rs),
[registry](../zensim/src/feature_defs.rs),
[research dispatch](../zensim/src/research.rs),
[bank/view/verifier tooling](../scripts/rev4_featpot/rev5_bank.py),
[diagnostic owner](../scripts/rev4_featpot/palette_diagnostic.py).
The existing zenanalyze palette tier counts occupied bins; it does not expose
weighted representative centres. The implementation reuses zensim's pixel
source and linear-sRGB conversion. Prior-art basis:
[OKLab](https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/) and
[Rubner et al., earth mover's distance](https://xenon.stanford.edu/~rubner/papers/rubnerIjcv00.pdf).
No verified palette-IQA derivation is claimed from the paper metadata search.
