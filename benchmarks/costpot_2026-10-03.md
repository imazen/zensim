# COSTPOT — extraction cost against feature potential for the top Rev4 feature sets (2026-10-03)

Measured 2026-10-03, 18:49–19:06 MT, on zensim main `37716b41` (includes KERNOPT2 `c510772d`). Development evidence; nothing
here is qualified. Artifact: <https://claude.ai/artifact/2Txv6HkRgvatCYGTQpvu8q> (private). Supersedes the Rev4 cost numbers
in `costset2_2026-10-03.md` and `costset3_2026-10-03.md`, which predate the Rev4 kernel fixes and ran on a loaded box.

Data: `costpot_2026-10-03.timing.tsv` (median/mean/min/MAD per run, size and arm) and `costpot_2026-10-03.potential.tsv`.
Raw zenbench JSONs, logs, load samples and the prep/assembly scripts: tower `output/zensim/costpot-2026-10-03/`.

## Method

* **Sets (21):** v2 + basic (E9″'s selection), the E10 basins, the COSTSET/E12 candidates, the E9″ near-miss additions
  (masked, c4), R0 (the 944 bank), single groups, and the external-read leaders. Group ranges: `v2_common.LEGACY_BLOCKS`
  and `rev4_featpot_partb_arms_amendment_2026-09-25.md`.
* **Potential:** `e13_teacher.score_arms` over the existing v2c cells, head N, seeds 0–4, five LODO folds (25 cells per set,
  0 missing) → `v2c/compare/costpot_lodo.json`. Δ = seed-paired change in held-out signed SROCC against v2 + basic;
  "as good" = E12's registered rule (mean Δ ≥ −0.002 and every source ≥ −0.005). External NITS/LIVE/MCIQA Δ are copied from
  `external_sets_e9_e10_e12_2026-10-03.json` (exploratory, open sets).
* **Cost:** one cell bake per set (`without_kadid_s0`, `selected_bake`), densified with `bake_dial_refit densify` (identity
  gate: BIT-IDENTICAL), then stamped Rev3 and Rev4 with `bake_stamp_revision` (cell bakes carry no revision stamp and would
  otherwise load as the legacy era). Cost depends on the declared feature IDs and revision, not the weights.
  `extract_paths_bench` models mode (`ZEN_XP_MODELS`), `cargo bench --features custom-profiles,feature-regime-v2,threads,training`,
  release without `target-cpu=native`, native tier (AVX-512), `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1`, `taskset -c 8`, `ZENSIM_FORMULA_REV`
  matching the stamp, synthetic pairs at 64², 256², 1024², 2048², all arms interleaved per size, 30 rounds (64², 256²) and
  25 rounds (1 MP, 4 MP). Score = `BakeScorer::compute`; steering map = `prepare_steering(.., 8)` worker `compute`.
  `ZEN_XP_CONTROLS=1` adds fast-ssim2 (single thread) and shipped profile D to the score runs.
* **Box:** dev (9950X3D), load average 1.8–3.4 on 32 threads throughout. zenbench marked every run UNRELIABLE because its
  strict gate (system CPU ≤ 5 %, no other heavy process) never cleared; the largest per-arm MAD was 3.9 % of the median.

## Steerability and servability (measured)

Steering session refusals (probe at 64²): masked/IW IDs f228–371 ("no spatial refinement") refuse append + masked,
v2 + basic + masked and R0; IDs ≥ f944 ("no integrand") refuse v2 + basic + c4, append + c4, v2 + csfw and b2 + c2.
13 of the 20 servable sets steer. `append + b2 + texgain` (E10 basin) cannot be served at all: texgain (f1825+) is not in
main's extraction plan (`densify` fails "bake reads features unavailable to the extraction plan").

## Reading

* **by_v2fy is the cheapest set that meets the "as good" rule** (0.53× score, 0.57× map at Rev4, steerable); b228_v2s123 also
  passes (0.60× / 0.78×). by_v2s123 is cheaper (0.40×) with the best mean but misses the KADID floor (−0.0077).
* **append + masked** ties on the mean and leads on W2, but costs 1.85× at Rev4 (1.37× at Rev3), misses the KADID floor and
  cannot be steered. The append block carries a Rev4 slowdown of its own: append alone is 88.9 ms at Rev4 vs 50.3 ms at Rev3
  (1.77×), against 1.24× for v2 + basic. E20's "replacement needs a cost measurement" is answered by this table.
* Additions to v2 + basic: + masked +0.0019 for 1.21× (unsteerable), + c4 +0.0014 for 5.55× (unsteerable).
* Rev4/Rev3: v2 + basic score 1.24× at 1 MP, 1.16× at 4 MP; the steering map costs the same at both revisions (124.2 vs
  126.2 ms at 1 MP; 1.08× at 4 MP).
* Not measured: the split between extraction and the MLP; why the f944+ families (c4, b2/c2, csfw) cost what they do
  (not profiled); real-image timing (synthetic pairs only).
