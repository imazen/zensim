## 0. Why this wave exists

Three lanes converge on one number:

1. **`era2_rank_preservation_2026-08-31.md` §4.3** — at radius 4, on *era-1
   weights*, the §21.1 bar passes on `C944` and `ADD156` and fails on `B`, both
   `W-LIN`s and `BHdr`. §4.5 says the FAILs are **upper bounds**, because every
   bake was fitted to a support width the candidate no longer has. **A retrain
   is the registered fix path** and this is it.
2. **`blur_radius_locality_branches_2026-08-31.md` §3.4** — a smaller radius is
   a *redistribution*, not a degradation, and it moves quality toward the axes
   the product is weakest on: KonJND gains **+0.0152** (C944, R5→R4) and
   monotonically more at R3/R2, while TID/KADID lose. KonJND is exactly the
   axis the ssim2 exam's closest candidate fails W1 on, by **−0.027**.
3. **`ssim2_replacement_bar_2026-08-31.md` §3.0 + APPENDIX A** — nobody passes
   the W1–W7 exam. The closest, `W10L9PH_s4004_packed`, fails **W1 on KonJND
   (−0.027)** and **W2's naming clause**.

So the wave's thesis, stated as a falsifiable claim: **radius 4 buys KonJND,
KonJND is W10L9PH's only W1 failure, and a retrain converts the other lanes'
radius FAILs from upper bounds into measured costs.** If the retrain does not
move KonJND, or moves it while breaking another axis, that is the finding.

## 4. THE BARS (frozen; none may be relaxed)

Three independent instruments, all owned elsewhere. This wave computes no
statistic of its own.

### 4.1 The era-2 §21.1 rank bar (the parent registration's own bar)

> **PASS iff no corpus loses more than `0.005` SROCC and the product composite
> does not fall.**

Applied per arm against **that arm's own era-1-trained counterpart**, over the
nine eval corpora, from `bake_verdict --regime 944 --full-json`. A retrain that
still fails moves radius 4 from "upper bound" to **measured cost** — which is
the information the flip decision needs, and is a publishable outcome.

### 4.2 The ssim2-replacement exam, W1–W7 (amended)

Verbatim from `ssim2_replacement_bar_2026-08-31.md` §2.3, with APPENDIX A's
amendment to W2 and the user's tightening of W4:

* **W1 (no regression).** On every held-out human corpus in R1 **and** R2, not
  worse than `peer_ssim2` by more than δ_corpus.
* **W2 (a real win).** Strictly better — paired 95 % CI excludes zero — on
  **≥ K = 2** axes, **≥ 1 of them CID22 or the near-lossless zone**.
  *AMENDED 2026-09-01 (APPENDIX A):* the named near-lossless corpus
  `hf_nearlossless` is an **ssim2 self-target** (`human_score == ssim2_gpu/100`
  exactly, 1200/1200) and is unwinnable at any width; the axis is
  **`hfnl_cid22band`** (CID22 top MOS band, n = 1425 over 49 refs, clustered
  bootstrap).
* **W3 (ladder).** Pooled material monotonicity ≥ ssim2's, **and** share of
  near-lossless ladders ending backwards ≤ ssim2's.
* **W4 (speed) — AMENDED TWICE on 2026-09-01; the governing text is
  AMENDMENT B2** (`benchmarks/hybrid_candidate_2026-09-01.md` §9.2, commit
  `5589ec99`), which supersedes both the original "≥ fast-ssim2 @1 T" and
  APPENDIX B.1's "≤ fast-ssim2 at 1 T **and** 8 T":

  > the candidate's mean ms/compare, priced on its OWN extraction regime plus
  > its OWN forwards, must be **≤ 1.25 × the 156-walk class, at BOTH 1 thread
  > and 8 threads** — where the 156-walk class is `add156_156basic` measured in
  > the same interleaved process, same images, same round. Both thread counts
  > bind. `fast-ssim2` is **retained as a context row** at both thread counts
  > but is **no longer the bar**. An ensemble is priced as ONE compare.

  The 1.25× is derived three ways (measurement resolution ~1.10× floor; class
  separation — the 372 fold sits at 1.27–1.43× and the 944 folds at 2.3–3.6×,

  construction).
* **W5 (HDR).** ≥ ssim2's integrated-PU path by δ_upiq, ssim2 measured in the

* **W6 (not circular).** S1–S3 (`nonphoto`/`imazen26`/`hfnlproxy`) ≥ 0.85 and

* **W7 (reachable).** The winning bytes load in a **default** build.
