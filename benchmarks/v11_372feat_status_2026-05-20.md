# V11-FEATURE-EXTRACT-372 — status (task #192, 2026-05-20)

## Goal

Re-extract zensim 372-feature vectors from the omni multi-codec R2 sweep,
then rebuild V11 substrate (anchor + cross-codec equivalence parquets) at
372-feat for downstream SOTA retrains.

The omni R2 sidecars shipped 300-feat zensim_features (no IW-pool block).
372-feat = 300 base + 72-col IW-pool block. The IW block contributed
+0.018-0.022 KADID SROCC vs 300-feat in prior 372-feat shipped bakes;
this substrate refresh enables that lift to compound with V11 substrate
gains in future trainings.

unresolvable without touching one of the sibling repos (out of scope
for this task).

- **Skipped this session**: zenavif (4,000) + zenjxl (51,200) =

## What's deferred to a follow-up session

The AVIF/JXL gap means this substrate refresh does NOT yet enable a
372-feat replacement of `cvvdp_iwssim_LARGE.parquet`. The cross-codec
training signal (the *point* of the substrate) requires all 4 codecs.

Three paths to close the gap:

1. **Resolve the zenjxl path conflict** in the zensim workspace
   `Cargo.toml`. Probably the right long-term fix; touches sibling
   repos. ~1-2 hr.
2. **Add `dav1d` + `jxl-oxide` as direct deps in zensim-bench**.
   Inverts the dep direction — zensim-bench would own its own
   AVIF/JXL decoders. Bypasses zen-metrics-cli. ~1 hr.
3. **Pre-decode AVIF/JXL via the existing `zen-metrics` BINARY**
   subprocess: shell out to `zen-metrics` for each AVIF/JXL pair to
   produce a PNG, then run the existing extractor. Disk-thrashy
   (~250 GB temp space for 55,200 1024² RGB PNGs) but no Cargo
   changes. ~2 hr. Could be made memory-only with a named-pipe but
   subprocess overhead dominates.

After the gap is closed, the remaining steps (per the task brief) are:

4. **Run substrate builder** to produce `anchors_ssim2_372col_v3.parquet`
   + `cross_codec_equivalence_ssim2_372col_v3.parquet`. ~5 min.
5. **5-seed CI retrain** of V11-A' Balanced at 372-feat with the new
   substrate using the recipe in the task brief
   (`--max-features 372 --target-column mix_cv35_iw65 --per-sample-alpha-head`
   etc.). ~1 hr GPU / 3 hr CPU per seed.
6. **Spline refit + Mohammadi panel** on all 6 corpora. ~10 min.
7. **Ship decision** vs V11 brief gate.
