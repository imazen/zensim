# CHROMAQ — chroma-quantization ladders: CVVDP, butteraugli and VMAF against zensim (2026-10-04)

Development diagnostic on unlabelled stimuli. It measures **agreement between metrics**, not agreement with people: no human rated
these images. Requested by the owner (2026-10-04): "generate distortion sets with zenjpeg manual color quantization controls and eval
them with cvvdp and butteraugli and vmaf and check agreement of 3 vs our model".

## Stimuli

* **References:** the 32 CLIC 2025 tuning photos (`codec-corpus/clic2025/training`, the CLIC "validation" set; ~2048 px long edge,
  8-bit sRGB). None is in a zensim human training or holdout set. 18 had an odd dimension and were cropped by one pixel (right/bottom)
  because VMAF needs even dimensions; no resampling. Manifest with source sha256: `chromaq_2026-10-04/src_manifest.json`.
* **Encoder:** zenjpeg through `zenmetrics sweep --codec zenjpeg` with the `plane_tables` knob: exact per-plane quantization tables =
  Annex K base matrix × multiplier `m = [y, cb, cr]` (`m = 0` = all-ones table, the finest JPEG allows), 16-bit DQT; optional
  `chroma_mask` coarsens only low (row+col ≤ 2) or only high (row+col > 2) chroma frequencies. Image
  `ghcr.io/imazen/zenfleet-worker:exec-cvvdp-0a61830d` (zenmetrics 0a61830d, image id `c1a97ef6…`). Grids:
  `chromaq_2026-10-04/grid444.json`, `grid420.json`; driver `run_sweep.sh`.
* **Families (4:4:4 unless noted; luma held at the all-ones table except `luma` and `mix_y1`):**
  `base` m=[0,0,0]; `luma` y ∈ {0.25…16}; `chroma` cb=cr ∈ {0.5…256}; `chroma_lf` and `chroma_hf` (same ladder, masked);
  `cb_only`, `cr_only` ∈ {2, 8, 32, 128}; `mix_y1` y=1 with cb=cr ∈ {1, 4, 16, 64}; `s420` 4:2:0 with cb=cr ∈ {0, 1, 4, 16, 64}.
  55 cells per image, 1,760 cells, 0 encode/decode/score failures. Encoded bytes on tower
  `output/zensim/chromaq-2026-10-04/enc/`.

## Scorers

* Judges (in the sweep): CVVDP (CPU port `cvvdp_cpu_imazen_v0_1_0`, display `standard_4k`), butteraugli (max and 3-norm), VMAF
  v0.6.1 (luma only), SSIMULACRA2, PSNR-Y (BT.709), and zensim's shipped profile through zenmetrics (`zensim_B`).
* Rev4 feature-set bakes (zensim `serve_custom_bake --pairs`, production `BakeScorer::compute`, `ZENSIM_FORMULA_REV=4`): featpot
  fold cells `without_aic3`, seeds 0–2, for by_v2fy@cv16:cf98 (`byv2fy`), v2+basic@cv16:cf98 (`v2basic`) and uncurated
  v2+basic (`v2basicU`); densified and stamped Rev4. These folds train on KADID, TID2013, KonFiG, CID22-A(25) and the coverage leg
  (AIC-3 held out). Set score = mean of the three seeds. Bake sha256s: `chromaq_2026-10-04/bakes.SHA256SUMS`.
* Second scoring with the R7 full-data confirm bakes (all exploratory sources, `refit/last.bin` of
  `confirm/cells/<entry>__N/full_s{0,1,2}`, densified + stamped Rev4; `chromaq_2026-10-04/bakes_full.SHA256SUMS`). Predictions on these
  unlabelled CLIC pairs read no sealed label. Both scorings are in the per-cell rows tables (block storage; `chromaq_2026-10-04/rows.pointer.md`).
* Analysis: `chromaq_2026-10-04/analyze.py` (per-image SROCC, cross-family concordance, monotonicity, consensus) and `exchange.py`
  (equivalent luma multiplier). Outputs `chromaq_2026-10-04/summary_{noaic3,full}.json`, `exchange_{noaic3,full}.json`.

## Results

All numbers below use the full-data bakes. The no-AIC-3 fold bakes agree within 0.01 on every agreement statistic; their reversal counts
(item 4) are up to 4 images higher (chroma 23–24/32).

**1. VMAF is not a clean negative control here.** zenjpeg holds JPEG's BT.601 Y fixed, but VMAF and PSNR-Y read BT.709 luma, so chroma
error leaks into their input (plus gamut clipping on decode): PSNR-Y ranges 23.6–63.8 dB and VMAF 58–100 across chroma-only cells. VMAF
still ranks chroma damage poorly: within-image SROCC with CVVDP 0.64 (minimum −0.21), and its ladders reverse often (75 upticks over 32
chroma ladders).

**2. Within-image ranking of chroma-only damage** (mean over 32 images, minimum in brackets):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| CVVDP vs butteraugli (reference) | — | 0.971 (0.930) | — | — |
| by_v2fy (full) | 0.932 | 0.930 | 0.936 | 0.933 (0.812) |
| v2 + basic (full) | 0.937 | 0.933 | 0.939 | — |

**3. Chroma against luma (the exchange rate).** Cross-family concordance, the share of (luma cell, chroma cell) pairs within an image that
a metric orders the same way as the judge (8,512 pairs): CVVDP vs butteraugli 0.891; by_v2fy 0.875 vs CVVDP, 0.836 vs butteraugli;
v2 + basic 0.882 / 0.838; SSIMULACRA2 0.875 / 0.841; zensim B 0.805 / 0.765. Equivalent luma multiplier (median log2; the luma step a
metric scores the same as the chroma cell):

| chroma step | CVVDP | butteraugli p3 | SSIMULACRA2 | zensim B | by_v2fy | v2 + basic |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| chroma ×4 | 0.69 | 1.04 | 0.77 | 0.50 | 0.77 | 0.60 |
| chroma ×8 | 1.48 | 2.23 | 1.68 | 1.20 | 1.55 | 1.56 |
| chroma ×16 | 2.53 | 3.44 | 2.48 | 1.70 | 2.27 | 2.26 |
| chroma ×32 | 3.47 | > 4 | 2.86 | 2.05 | 2.45 | 2.59 |
| chroma ×128 (grey) | > 4 | > 4 | 2.32 | 0.35 | 2.01 | 2.17 |
| Cb only ×32 | 2.38 | 3.19 | 2.17 | 0.30 | 1.38 | 1.40 |
| Cr only ×32 | 2.81 | > 4 | 2.14 | 1.81 | 2.05 | 2.13 |

(> 4 = worse than luma ×16, the coarsest luma step.) Up to chroma ×16 the Rev4 bakes put chroma damage where CVVDP does; butteraugli
weights chroma more than either. Beyond ×16 the bakes flatten near luma ×5–6 while CVVDP and butteraugli keep falling. zensim B
under-weights chroma throughout, and Cb most of all.

**4. The desaturation reversal.** With every chroma coefficient zeroed (chroma ×128, a grey image), the score is HIGHER than at chroma ×32
(posterized colour) in 20/32 images for by_v2fy, 21 for v2 + basic, 29 for zensim B and 26 for SSIMULACRA2; CVVDP 1/32, butteraugli 0/32.
Cr only: 14 / 16 / 29 / 23 against 6 and 1; Cb only: 10 / 9 / 23 / 13 against 2 and 0. Whether people prefer a grey image to badly
posterized colour is not settled by any data here; it is the case where the metrics split by design.

**5. by_v2fy vs v2 + basic on chroma.** Indistinguishable here (agreement within 0.005; the same exchange rate within 0.25 log2 steps),
although by_v2fy carries no full-resolution X/B columns. The NITS chromatic-noise shift and the KonJND-JPEG deficit from the sealed read
are not visible as a chroma-quantization difference.

## Limits

* Agreement with CVVDP and butteraugli is not agreement with people. Both are colour-aware models with their own assumptions; they
  agree with each other at 0.97, which bounds how much "agreement" can mean.
* One codec family (per-plane JPEG tables), 4:4:4 except the 4:2:0 ladder, one display model for CVVDP (`standard_4k`), 32 photos.
  No screen content, no palette quantization (GIF/PNG-8 style), no dithering.
* The chroma ladder saturates at ×128 (identical outputs at ×128 and ×256), which inflates tie counts in the monotonicity statistics.
