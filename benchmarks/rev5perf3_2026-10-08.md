## Reproduced failures

The new row fixtures use guaranteed-aligned u128 storage for trimmed references, and vary padding and byte offsets on candidate rows while retaining identical pixel bytes. They cover RGB8, RGBA8, BGRA8, RGBA16 and f32 RGBA, every residue modulo bytes-per-pixel, full extra-pixel padding, offsets zero/one, odd dimensions, alpha modes, sRGB/P3/BT.2020, clipping/preserved gamut, serial/parallel conversion, output padding, chunk heights, revisions 1–5, subset views, SDR/HDR strip producers and public scoring.

Before the fix, six gates fail with `OutputSliceWouldHaveSlop` or `TargetAlignmentGreaterAndInputNotAligned`: XYB, Y′CbCr, native SDR, HDR conversion, public scoring and subset/strip production. The width-indexed identity/reflection/delta/palette readers pass. The full failing output is retained at `/mnt/v/output/zensim/rev5perf3-2026-10-08/before/provenance/padded-rows.log`.

The audit found whole-row u8 casts in both gamut branches of packed RGB/RGBA/BGRA conversion and Y′CbCr, whole-row f32 casts in native SDR, packed conversion and HDR strip input, and a whole-row u16 cast in Y′CbCr. Descriptor-HDR nits extraction already slices the pixel prefix but assumes f32 alignment. `ImageSource` and `StridedBytes` permit byte strides with no such alignment guarantee. Width-indexed readers, reflect-padding and PQ-u16 decoding already read only pixel bytes.
