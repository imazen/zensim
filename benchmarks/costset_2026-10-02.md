# COSTSET — cheaper feature sets as good as v2 + basic

Registered 2026-10-02 (Mountain Time, night), before any fit. Brief: `~/tmp/zensim-paper/rev4/COSTSET_brief.md`. Lane `steercheck`.
Results are appended below once measured.

## Registration
Recipe `benchmarks/costset_2026-10-02.recipe.json` (same strict train/eval route, segments, seeds 5101/5103/5107, H128, 32 epochs as
`steercheck_2026-10-02`): 16 arms, all inside the steering-supported IDs f0-227 and f372-719. References: `basic228`, `basic156`, `v2basic`.
Candidates vary what the planner can really skip (per `feature_plan`): v2 scales (`v2_scales` mask), v2 channels, basic fine-Y (X/B only
at scales >= 1), peaks present or not. Planner facts read from source before registering: `gradient`, `blockiness` and `transducer_bank`
are switched on together with `v2_blocks`, so omitting individual v2 slots inside a scale saves no work; whole v2 scales are skipped
(`v2_scales`); whether the X/B channels at scale 0 are skipped for v2 is not established by the source read and is measured below.
Cost is measured on the trained bakes (scalar and prepared map, 1 MP and 4 MP, single thread CPU 8, interleaved); rank is the owner's human
eval MAE/SROCC (development evidence); steering is the broad 96 + owner 12 through the prepared path.
Naming: `b156`/`b228`/`by` = basic 156 / core 228 / basic with X/B only at scales >= 1; `v2sNNN` = v2 at those scales (all channels),
`v2fy` = v2 Y-only at scale 0 and all channels at scales 1-3, `v2yall` = v2 Y channel only at every scale.

## Results (2026-10-02, Mountain Time, night)

**Plainly: nothing cheaper matches v2 + basic on all three axes.** The closest cheaper sets give up either a little rank or 8 of the 96 broad
steering passes, and the biggest cost lever (v2 scale 0) cannot be had without losing both. Development evidence on Rev3 tables; the eval pairs
were already used in selection; nothing is qualified.

### Setup and honesty limits
* 17 arms (`costset_2026-10-02.recipe.json`, committed before any fit as a222346): 14 candidates + references `basic228`, `basic156`, `v2basic`.
  Three seeds (5101/5103/5107), strict train/eval owner route, H128 / 32 epochs. MAE columns are 3-seed medians with [min-max]; adjacent differences
  below about 0.3 MAE are inside seed spread (v2basic itself spans 8.13-8.52).
* Steering: broad 96 + owner 12 through the **prepared** path (STEERAPI), uniform three-seed ensembles for the broad panel.
* Cost: `extract_paths_bench` models mode, release without target-cpu=native, `ZENSIM_FORMULA_REV=3`, single thread pinned to CPU 8, all 17 arms interleaved
  in one zenbench group per size, 30 rounds at 1 MP and 25 at 4 MP. **The box was loaded** (load average 22 at the scalar run's start, 18 at its end; 18 -> 19 for
  the prepared run; fleet workers on the other cores) and zenbench flagged both runs UNRELIABLE (resource-gate waits). Interleaving cancels drift between arms but not
  the absolute level: the prepared v2basic here is 233 ms at 1 MP against 180 ms measured earlier on a quieter box. Read the ratios, not the milliseconds.
  Per-arm min/median/MAD are in the JSON the bench wrote (`/var/tmp/steercheck/csbench/*.json`, not committed); `costset_2026-10-02.results.json` carries the means.

### Where the cost is (item 1)
* **Planner fact (measured, not just read):** the planner skips v2 work only by whole pyramid scale (`v2_scales`). Omitting v2 channels or slots saves nothing:
  `b156_v2yall` (v2 Y channel only, all scales), `b156_v2fy` and `by_v2fy` (X/B skipped at scale 0) all cost the same as full v2 (scalar 85.7 vs 85.5 ms at 1 MP).
  Basic fine-Y (`by_*`) saves only when v2 is absent (`by_only` 18.2 ms vs `basic156` 29.6) and nothing with v2 present (`by_v2s123` = `b156_v2s123`).
  Peaks cost nothing in the scalar path (`basic228` = `basic156`) but +20 ms (1 MP) / +76 ms (4 MP) in the prepared map. A cheaper ID list that only drops slots saves no work.
* **By pyramid scale** (b156 + v2 at scales S, scalar ms 1 MP / 4 MP; prepared ms 1 MP / 4 MP):
  basic156 29.6 / 146.0; 69.8 / 258.6 | + v2 s3: 33.9 / 165.8; 135.2 / 534.5 | + s2-3: 33.9 / 165.0; 138.6 / 544.0 | + s1-3: 44.3 / 211.7; 164.2 / 616.8 |
  + s0-3 (v2basic): 85.5 / 396.1; 232.8 / 922.7. **Scale 0 is 41 of v2's 56 scalar ms (74%) and ~70 ms of its map cost; scales 1, 2, 3 add 10, 0 and 4 scalar ms.**
* **By sub-pass** (`v2_feature_group_cost`, 1 MP, ST CPU 8, v2 walk with the v1 basic included, toggles off -> on; load average 19; 30 rounds):
  none of the three toggle groups 57.3 ms | + gradient group (GMS, ringing, banding, edge width) 63.3 (+6.0, +10.5%) | + transducer bank 57.9 (+0.6, +1%) |
  + blockiness 59.9 (+2.6, +4.5%) | all three 66.9 (+9.6, +16.8%). The three groups are 17% of the v2 walk; the rest (SSIM deviation pools, ART/DET/MSE/HF,
  masked/IW pools, soft peaks) is the dense kernel at every scale and is not separable by toggle. Per-channel cost is not separable at all (no channel skipping).
* **The prepared map has a large fixed v2 overhead:** v2 at scale 3 only adds 4 ms to the scalar path but 65 ms (1 MP) / 276 ms (4 MP) to the prepared map; the map
  cost therefore falls only 29% when scale 0 is dropped (233 -> 164 ms) against 48% for the scalar path. The planner skip does not reach the session's retention walk; changing
  that is outside this lane's files (attribution pass B / session retention are REV4SERVE territory), asked in `COSTSET_decisions.md`.

### Which candidates are cheaper and how good (relative to v2basic)
| arm | scalar 1MP/4MP | prepared 1MP/4MP | dSROCC | dMAE | dBroad |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| b228_v2s123 | 0.52 / 0.53 | 0.77 / 0.75 | +0.0000 | +0.18 | -8 |

| b156_v2s23 | 0.40 / 0.42 | 0.60 / 0.59 | -0.0071 | +0.28 | -12 |
| basic156 (no v2) | 0.35 / 0.37 | 0.30 / 0.28 | -0.0056 | +0.44 | -5 |

Reading: **dropping v2 scale 0 halves the scalar cost** (0.52x) and cuts the prepared map by 23-35%; it keeps SROCC within 0.005 of v2basic (b228_v2s123 matches it to 4
places), gives up 0.2-0.5 MAE, and costs 0-14 broad steering passes depending on the set. The two best trade-offs: **b228_v2s123** (rank-equal by SROCC, +0.18 MAE, 80/96) and
**b156_v2s123_y** (88/96 = steering equal, SROCC -0.0024, MAE +0.38, cheapest steering-equal set: 0.52x scalar, 0.65x prepared). `v2only` shows basic columns are not needed for rank
(+0.001 SROCC) and saves 24% of the prepared map at unchanged scalar cost, but loses 14 steering passes. Steering pass counts move by up to +-6 between sets with
no structure that the grid explains (e.g. `b228_v2fy` 91 vs `b156_v2fy` 82); treat differences below ~6 passes as noise.
