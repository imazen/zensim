## Findings

1. **Cell 5 is the BVLS-linear optimum.** No recipe beats its mean-g3
   (0.8145) by more than measurement noise. `ssim2_noconc` ties at
   +0.0004 (within noise). The linear family is saturated.
2. **ssim2_gpu is the right training target** — by a wide margin. cvvdp
   (−0.080) and iwssim (−0.095) targets crater the panel: those metrics
   correlate less with human MOS than ssim2 on the safesyn distribution.
3. **mix_ss_iw (50/50 ssim2+iwssim distance target) is the CID22 SROCC
   record: 0.8743** — beats Cell 5 (0.8703) and v47 (0.8657) on the
   gold-standard compression holdout. Reproducible across both rounds.
   Costs KonJND (0.4644 vs Cell 5's 0.4904), so it's a CID22 specialist,
   not an all-rounder. Saved as a candidate:
   `/mnt/v/output/zensim/bakes/v02_372feat_mix_ss_iw_2026-05-29.bin`
   (sha256 53f139c2…).
4. **Multi-corpus training HURTS** (−0.061). V0_2's "safesyn-only,
   single-source target" wisdom holds at 372 features — adding
   cid22_train+kadid+tid drags the panel because the mixed human_score
   scales add noise the linear projection can't reconcile.
5. **Low-q weighting + soft concordance both slightly hurt** the panel.
   The hard ±2-bucket concordance filter and uniform row weighting are
   already near-optimal.

## What would break the ceiling (not done — needs greenlight)

- **Nonlinearity (small MLP).** v47 (372→128→64 MLP) reaches CID22
  0.8657 but loses the all-rounder to Cell 5 — MLP trades panel balance
  for capacity. A SMALL MLP (hidden 2–4) trained the V0_2 way might land
  between, but the trainer source is currently entangled in a large
  uncommitted f64→f32 port WIP in the working tree; running it cleanly
  needs that WIP resolved first.
- **New features.** The 372-feature vector is the ceiling for the linear
  fit. Genuinely new signal (e.g. the localized-defect / tile-min head
  from #33, or butteraugli-style opponent-color features) would be
  needed to move the panel.
- **Butter-target bake for regression detection** — a DIFFERENT
  objective from the human-MOS panel (won't improve panel SROCC). The
  extractor now has a `--corpus pairs` mode + 372 features extracted for
  the corruption corpus, so this is unblocked — but the corpus is one
  reference image (gb82_dog), too narrow to train a general
  butter-approximator without broader butter-scored data.

## Verdict

The linear search is **exhausted**: Cell 5 stands as the best all-rounder,
mix_ss_iw is the CID22 champion. Both are 4.8 KB pure-linear bakes.
Shipping mix_ss_iw as a CID22-specialist sibling is a judgment call
(it wins the single most important compression holdout but isn't a
balanced win) — flagged for user decision.
