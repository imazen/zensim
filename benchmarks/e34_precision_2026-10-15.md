## Served-map trace and implementation

**Yes, the served map consumes the same FD sensitivities.** `SteeringSession::compute` (`zensim/src/metric/bake.rs:1612`) supplies the prepared serving plan. `compute_attribution_input` selects `prepared_steering_gradient` at line 1434. That calls `score_features_fd_gradient` (line 582), which calls `fd_gradient_into` (line 764) with the registered step. The all-zero/below-floor recovery at line 655 uses the same routine on the pre-floor owner. `candidate_map_sensitivities` (line 1449) feeds map construction, and the original sensitivities are retained in `ScoredAttribution`. `refinement_gain` (`zensim/src/attribution.rs:5693`) uses that map and passes those sensitivities to the exact-neighbour weighted gain at line 5712. Exact neighbour feature deltas therefore still carry FD weights.

The test-only [reference and packet](../zensim/src/metric/steerfix_precision.rs) is included by `steerfix_packet.rs` under `cfg(all(test, steerfix_instrument))`.
