   pre-registered on. Method (mix weights, solver, screen treatment, verdict
   tool, Σ definition) replicates e2 exactly; the archived-bin re-runs prove
   today's toolchain reproduces e2's numbers where the inputs are e2's.
4. KADID/TID stay train-guards; the clean-holdout-only Σ (−0.045) and the
   guard-inclusive Σ (−0.069) agree in sign and rough magnitude.
5. Zero-ablation LOO measures marginal value *within this BVLS fit* (no
   refit) — identical semantics to the e2 numbers it is compared against.
6. The e2-era LOO deltas quoted for calibration were re-derived from the
   archived bins with today's stats code — they match the published table to
   ≤0.0005, so stats drift since 2026-07-23 is nil on these panels.
