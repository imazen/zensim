zensim 0.3.0 profile A: verified evaluations and runtime benchmarks

zensim-a is a full-reference perceptual image similarity metric built for codec quality control, encoder steering, and visual regression testing. It converts both images to XYB across four dyadic scales, extracts 492 local damage features plus 492 reference-fragility products, and feeds a 128-unit network certified monotone in its damage inputs before a 21-knot monotone PCHIP spline (100 = identical, below 0 for severe damage).

In the shipped unpruned bake, one tied max pair carries 99.998% of the mean raw score contribution (approximately two additive damage budgets, plus a 31-unit residual with output weight −0.000031). Monotonicity holds in the damage features up to floating-point rounding, not across every pixel edit or close encoder step. The per-region steering map (compute_with_diffmap) uses a separate fixed 156-coefficient projection.

1. Registered frozen-A final assessment

Registered on 2026-11-05 and read once per population on the frozen 0.3 development bake without changing weights, calibration, or acceptance thresholds. Comparators ran on identical decoded RGB8 pixels; Butteraugli quality is explicitly −ba_p3. Signed values preserve target polarity (positive on quality targets, negative on distortion targets); RMSE stays in each dataset’s native unit and is never averaged across collections. Marginal 95% intervals resample whole reference images and are not paired significance tests.

KADID-10k TERMINAL failed both registered confirmation rules. The rules compared frozen A against two internal research models (formula-5 B and formula-4 seed-0 epoch-119 by_v2fy, served via its approved metadata-only revision tag) rather than published metrics. Both comparators trained on KADID’s other references while A did not train on KADID; that difference is disclosed, not proved to explain the gap. The failure stands unchanged.

5. Engineering qualification gates, JPEG XL steering, and HDR front end

Rank correlation on MOS collections does not verify dial monotonicity, SIMD determinism, or spatial quantization steering. Model selection enforced five engineering gates alongside human-rating panels; all imperfect pass rates and byte costs are retained below.

Dial & determinism gates

Certified-monotone head + ladder checks

Dial contract C1–C6 (monotone along ladders, no flat zones, negative tail, exact 100 on identical images, nothing above an identical copy) passed across seeds 0–2 on both evaluation grids, and exact identity passed across 12 x86 SIMD tier permutations. On near-identity ladders, 132 / 144 were monotone per seed. Across 646,677 held-out close encoder-ladder pairs where Butteraugli and CVVDP agree, A still misorders 3.39% of pairs (F3: 3.35%).

Spatial maps & JPEG XL loop

156-coefficient map under independent judges

Chroma level (worst MAE ratio 1.008 vs bar 1.05) and chroma trade misordering (2.47% vs F3 2.50%) passed. Seed 0 passed 133 / 135 regional map-steering cases (one text-screenshot case read 0.69 against a 0.70 bar at every seed). In a closed JPEG XL quantization loop against F3, the reported byte-delta intervals include zero (+0.19% [−0.09, +0.46] bytes by SSIMULACRA2; −0.21% [−0.56, +0.12] by Butteraugli), whereas against a Butteraugli-steered loop, zensim’s map costs +2.18% bytes under SSIMULACRA2 and +5.31% under Butteraugli.

Zero-shot HDR continuity

Anchored cube root at 203 cd/m² SDR white

compute_hdr maps linear, PQ, or HLG light through an anchored cube-root front end without HDR human training rows. An SDR image presented as HDR at 203 cd/m² scores within 0.0003 points of its SDR score on the research panel. On UPIQ, zero-shot A reads SROCC 0.943 on the 50 development rows (vs 0.835 with an earlier PU21 front end) and 0.811 across all 380 UPIQ rows. HDR scores are not a calibrated human scale.

6. Runtime latency, parallel cutoff, and process memory

Measured on an Intel Core Ultra 7 270K Plus (x86-64 AVX2/v3, CPUs 0–15: 8P + 8E, no CCD boundary): 20 real images per size, zenjpeg q40 YCbCr 4:2:0 reconstructions, resident RGB8 sRGB bytes scored in-process (decode, encode, resize, I/O, and CLI startup excluded), 100 ms warmup, first 32 clean interleaved zenbench rounds per image (640 calls per arm/cell), type-7 quantiles, thin LTO, no -C target-cpu=native. Comparators are registry fast-ssim2 0.9.1 and the unchanged in-process margarine simd-row-malta kernel (primary max pooling; runtime baseline, not an accuracy comparator). Does not measure Zen 5 AVX-512, ARM NEON, Wasm, or GPU.

Pre-cutoff small-image caveat: All eight comparative rows below were measured before shipping the 36,864-padded-pixel (192²) serial cutoff. At 64², A is slower than fast-ssim2 at 1 thread (0.75 ms vs 0.40 ms) and suffered Rayon overhead at 16 threads (2.15 ms pre-cutoff; 0.70 ms after forcing serial below 192²). At 12 MP (12.00–12.58M pixels), 16-thread A scores in 105.4 ms p50 (3.39× faster than fast-ssim2, 5.22× faster than margarine), while margarine’s tiled row buffers hold lower 16-thread total-process peak RSS (167,168 KiB vs A’s 598,216 KiB).

8. Known limits, primary records, and citations

Summary of established limits

  • KADID TERMINAL confirmation failed against both registered research comparators (B and by_v2fy), despite leading SSIMULACRA2 and Butteraugli on the same 2,000 pairs.
  • Global tone, gamut, and geometric edits remain weak across all tested FR metrics: NITS-IQA D4 contrast change reads 0.22 for A (best tested metric: 0.46); KADID color-block (0.489), non-eccentricity patch (0.500), and pixelate (0.577) show similar local-metric limits; CID:IQ SGCK gamut mapping reads 0.87 vs F3’s 0.91.
  • Close learned-codec and ladder ordering: on CLIC 2021 pairs within 2.4 points, A leans toward smoother reconstructions where raters prefer fine detail; 3.39% of close encoder-ladder pairs remain misordered despite feature-level monotonicity.
  • Spatial steering vs Butteraugli: one text-screenshot map case reads 0.69 vs 0.70, and closed-loop JPEG XL quantization steered by zensim’s 156-coefficient map uses 2.18%–5.31% more bytes than a Butteraugli-steered loop at matched judge scores.
  • Small images, HDR, and restricted sets: images below 64 px are reflect-padded (where SSIMULACRA2 is invalid); HDR scores depend on declared white/boost and are not a calibrated human scale; committee-only benchmark labels are excluded from public reports.
