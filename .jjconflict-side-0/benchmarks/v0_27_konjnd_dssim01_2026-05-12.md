## Verdict — dssim co-training is FALSIFIED for cycle-7

V0_27 vs V0_26 (just adding dssim_w=0.1):
- CID22: +0.002 (noise)
- **JPEG-AI: -0.060 (catastrophic regression)**

- Other AIC-4: -0.013 to -0.013

V0_27 vs V0_16:
- JPEG-AI: -0.016 (V0_27 is WORSE than V0_16 on JPEG-AI, despite
  dssim's own JPEG-AI score being 0.9147)

**Mechanism**: dssim head pushes V_X toward dssim's quality scale
on SYNTH rows (which are dominated by JPEG-class artifacts and don't
include JPEG-AI encodings). dssim and ssim2 agree mostly on JPEG;
dssim's separate "transformer-codec friendliness" isn't transferable
through a synth-corpus auxiliary head. Training V_X on dssim's
JPEG-shape opinions DILUTES the KonJND-induced JPEG-AI sensitivity.

**The correct path to JPEG-AI**: add JPEG-AI training samples to
the synth corpus. The cycle-7 hypothesis that "dssim auxiliary head
captures JPEG-AI structure" is now disproven empirically.
